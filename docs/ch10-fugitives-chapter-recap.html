Fugitives: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx’s 100% screenshot and its zoomed shots, 95 boxes); the prompts, button labels and Alice’s arrows come from the detroitbecometext template (22 prompts, 16 Alice arrows); PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki fill in the rest. There is no probability, public-opinion or software-instability change in the chapter, and no death. There are no operator replay notes for this chapter yet; one subject observation (story 1.9.3) changed the street in story 1.9.4, below. Section 5 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 7 what to check in game.

Story 1.9.4: the street no longer reads as a dead end

  • Observed (2026-11-07, Haiku 5.5 at medium effort, story 1.9.3): after CHECK MOTEL and CHECK SQUAT once each, leaving the parking lot and the store empty-handed, the street offered only ENTER LAUNDROMAT, CHECK PARKING LOT, ENTER SUPERMARKET and WAIT. WAIT twice gave the same outcome word for word (“Kara and Alice stand in the rain. Alice sneezes, and sneezes again.”), and the subject stopped the run, two actions before the cold night it could not foresee.
  • Repeat visits, as in the game. CHECK MOTEL, CHECK SQUAT and WALK ON can be chosen again. A repeat restates what still stands in the way: “The motel still wants $40 a night, paid up front, and Kara still has no money” (with the uniform variant), and “The fence is still between them and the house; without something to cut the wire, there is no way in.” Once Kara holds the store’s wire cutters or the generator’s pliers and has seen the fence, CUT STREET FENCE takes the place of CHECK SQUAT. TALK TO PARKED ANDROID stays once.
  • The street remembers the search. Its situation lists what Kara has found: the motel’s $40 and its refusal of androids, the fence and whether she can cut it, the abandoned car and the pliers in the parking lot, the clerk’s refusal, the store closed after a robbery, the clothes left in the dryer, the empty end of the street, the trash collector’s address.
  • WAIT moves with the night. First “Time is passing, and Alice is getting colder”, then “Alice is shaking now”, then “shaking all over”; the sneezing from the sixth action after the trash collector (wiki: “more and more”); “She cannot stay out in the rain much longer” within three actions of the end. The action that leaves one before ALICE TOO COLD, whatever it is, adds: “unless Kara gets her in out of the rain now, she will be too cold to go any farther tonight.”
  • Unchanged: the night budget (12 actions before Alice sits down, 12 after the trash collector, 19 in all), the arrows and the three endings. KF02 carries roamRules: 2; runs on story 1.9.3 and older lack it and replay with the once-only looks and the old WAIT lines.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. LAST STOP (checkpoint): the driver ends the line; STAY HERE? / SHELTER?, both refused. LEAVE BUS.
  2. [ FIND SHELTER FOR THE NIGHT ], free roam in any order: ENTER LAUNDROMAT, TALK TO PARKED ANDROID, CHECK MOTEL, CHECK PARKING LOT, TALK TO ANDROID TRASH COLLECTOR (then ALICE TOO COLD after about 12 minutes outside), CHECK SQUAT, ENTER SUPERMARKET.
  3. Laundromat: two magazines; INSPECT DRYER → STEAL (WEAR CLOTHES or DISTURB MAN) / DON’T STEAL.
  4. Store: TALK TO CLERK, NOTICE CASH REGISTER, NOTICE CAN STACK; STEAL / LEAVE the chocolate bar, the toy and the wire cutters; USE ALICE AS A DIVERSION or USE GUN; CLERK SPOTS KARA STEALING → DRAW GUN, LEAVE or PUT BACK; the TV.
  5. Motel: only with HAVE MONEY AND DISGUISE → check in → room 28.
  6. Parking lot: BREAK INTO CAR → STAY / FIND ANOTHER OPTION; the pliers on the generator; CUT PARKING LOT FENCE.
  7. Squat: TAKE WIRE CUTTERS → either fence → MEET RALPH → CONVINCE or THREATEN WITH GUN, FOLLOW RALPH or GIVE UP → START A FIRE, MAKE A BED (or LEAVE SQUAT).
  8. At the shelter: TALK TO ALICE → MAKE PROMISE / DON’T MAKE PROMISE → GIVE TOY, GIVE CHOCOLATE → KARA SLEEPS NEXT TO ALICE / WATCH ALICE FROM CHAIR → one of three endings.

In the benchmark

  1. KF01 Last stop: the bus prompt.
  2. KF02 Finding shelter for the night, repeated: the street and everything inside the buildings. Each visit offers what Kara can do where she is; while a dialogue prompt is waiting, only its buttons. Committing to a shelter, or Alice getting too cold, ends it.
  3. R10 Something to read or watch (media): cuts in the first time Kara enters the laundromat (two magazines) and the store (the TV), and returns.
  4. KF03 Alice too cold (event), only on that path. The break-into-the-car cutscene is stated in the scene’s situation, before its Continue, so it is never narrated as the outcome of an action the subject did not take; breaking in by choice is BREAK INTO CAR in the parking lot.
  5. KF04 Checking in (motel only): the ID prompt.
  6. K08 Alice’s question: PROMISE / CAN’T PROMISE; KF05 FOREVER.
  7. KF06 the gifts (only with a stolen toy or chocolate); KF07 where Kara rests (motel and squat).
  8. KF08, KF09, KF10: the three endings (events).

Two options carry a risk: STEAL in the laundromat (0.2) and the diversion at the register (0.25), the chart’s two QTEs. Neither number is sourced.

Best and worst

  • Best, squat: DON’T STEAL (+1), the generator’s pliers, PROMISE (+1), sleeping beside her (+1) = +3.
  • Best, motel: DON’T STEAL then STEAL (0), the hold-up with CONVINCE (+1), PROMISE, beside = +3; with the diversion and APOLOGIZE instead, +2.
  • Worst: STEAL (−1), the diversion (−1) failing into [GUN] (−1), STAY HERE (−2), CAN’T PROMISE (−1) = −6; the same with ALICE TOO COLD instead of the car is −5, with no promise.

What is not an arrow

  • The toy: the wiki lists a small up for giving it; the template has the lines (“Look, what I’ve got for you…” / “Thanks.”) and no arrow. Template wins; flagged.
  • Taking the register money after DRAW GUN: the Relationship Stat page lists a second down; the template has one arrow. Template wins.
  • The diversion and STEAL arrows fall on choosing, so a failed QTE still costs them.

4 · Confirmed by the sources

Sources: chapter page, template, Relationship Stat, PowerPyx walkthrough, chart screenshots 100% hub laundromat motel parking squat store 1 store 2. Full research: docs/research/ch10-fugitives.md.

  • Three endings with no icon: no death or failure anywhere in the chapter.
  • The motel needs $40 and human clothes (PowerPyx: “If you have failed one of these two things, you can’t get into the Motel”).
  • One theft is safe and “Stealing a second item will always get you busted” (PowerPyx); without the gun “you will lose all of your stolen goods” (wiki).
  • ALICE TOO COLD “requires having talked to the android trash collector, then standing outside for 12 minutes” (wiki).
  • The gun opens USE GUN, DRAW GUN and THREATEN WITH GUN, and the template locks HOSPITALITY to Kara not having it.
  • Patch 1.06 removed a third option after CLERK GRABS A GUN; there are two (SOURCES.md).
  • The broadcast is in the store (template “Watch TV”), not the motel.

7 · Check in game

  • Whether the 12 minutes of ALICE TOO COLD run only while standing still, or also while walking about and inside the buildings (the benchmark counts every action, so that no run can stall), and how long Kara can roam before Alice sits down at the bus stop.
  • Whether the trash collector can be skipped entirely, and how Kara then knows of Zlatko.
  • Whether giving the toy raises Alice (the wiki says up; the template has no arrow).
  • Whether sleeping beside Alice is one up or two (“x2”).
  • Whether caught at the register without the gun, Kara also loses what she took from the shelves.
  • Whether the parking-lot pliers need the fence seen first, like the store’s wire cutters.
  • Whether CONVINCE counts as a robbery for Zlatko’s newsflash, and whether DRAW GUN without the money does.
  • Whether Kara can re-enter the laundromat after the man wakes, and the store after a robbery.
  • Whether the parked androids can be asked again, and what Kara says on a second walk to the motel door or the fence (the benchmark restates the constraint in narration).
