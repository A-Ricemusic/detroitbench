A reference for replaying the chapter. 1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. INVESTIGATE EDEN CLUB (checkpoint) → FOLLOW HANK → ON CRIME SCENE → LISTEN TO BRIEFING. Hank is hungover; Gavin calls the victim “some pervert who got more action than he could handle.”
  2. [ SEARCH FOR CLUES ]: INVESTIGATE VICTIM (GRAHAM, MICHAEL; SEVERE BRUISING; CARDIAC ARREST), INVESTIGATE ANDROID; the blue blood and the wallet off the chart.
  3. REPAIR ANDROID → TALK TO ANDROID: four prompts before she shuts down → AGGRESSIVE APPROACH or CALM APPROACH.
  4. LEAVE CRIME SCENE (checkpoint). Idle too long and Hank wants to leave: CONTINUE or GIVE UP → MISSION FAILED.
  5. FIND AN ANDROID EYEWITNESS (the booth facing the door) → ASK HANK TO HELP (he rents her, $29.99) → PURSUE DEVIANT through the rented androids’ memories, or ANDROIDS’ MEMORIES ARE RESET → MISSION FAILED.
  6. ENTER THE WAREHOUSE (checkpoint) → [ SEARCH FOR BLUE HAIRED DEVIANT ]: two magazines, ANALYZE BLUE BLOOD, INVESTIGATE ANDROID STOCK, FIND RA9 SYMBOL; GIVE UP → MISSION FAILED.
  7. INVESTIGATE BLUE HAIRED DEVIANT → DEVIANTS ATTACK → WIN FIGHT or LOSE FIGHT.
  8. Won: SHOOT DEVIANT → DEVIANT KILLS HERSELF WITH CONNOR’S GUN → DEVIANTS DIED, or SPARE DEVIANT → DEVIANTS ESCAPED. Lost: SHOOT DEVIANTS IN THE BACK → CONNOR SHOT DEVIANTS, or LET DEVIANTS GO → DEVIANTS ESCAPED.

In the benchmark

  1. C12a: one narrated event.
  2. C12s1…C12s7: a hub of up to seven actions (one scene per action); the last option reactivates the Traci once the neck, the heart and the android are examined.
  3. C12t1…C12t4: the four prompts with the game’s labels; the approach node is recorded after the fourth.
  4. C12l: the checkpoint, narrated. C12e1…C12e3: the club floor hub; C12g: Hank’s CONTINUE / GIVE UP, if he has not been called within three actions; C12e4, C12e5: the extra minute.
  5. C12p1…C12p6, each with a second try C12pNr: six hops of “which android next”; the fourth wrong probe is the reset.
  6. C12w0: the checkpoint, narrated; R20: the magazine; C12w1…C12w5: the warehouse hub, five actions.
  7. C12f1 (storage room) and C12f2 (alley): two risk rolls. Losing either is LOSE FIGHT.
  8. C12 (won) and C12x (lost): SHOOT or SPARE.

Hubs are unrolled: each action is its own scene, open only while the hub lasts, because the shared regression test allows only the earlier chapters’ repeatable scenes to repeat. The subject sees a new opaque ID per action.

5 · Where the benchmark simplifies

  • Prompt 2’s VICTIM is not offered: its only content is marked unused in the template. The string table’s OTHER GIRL and ANDROID buttons for prompt 4 are not in the template either.
  • The cut lines are not built: the manager’s “how long’s this gonna take?”, Hank’s “You’re really reading their memories…”, and the blue-haired Traci’s “What am I supposed to do without her?!”.
  • The approach node: AGGRESSIVE if DIRECT, MURDERER or PRESSURE was used; EXPLAIN counts as calm (unconfirmed).
  • The memory chain’s route is one reader’s working sequence; each hop’s direction is the game’s HUD conclusion; the two wrong androids offered at each hop are invented.
  • The warehouse corner needs the blue blood first (PowerPyx, GamerGuides); one reader says it does not. The second blood serial (#354 411 057) is placed in the warehouse by inference.
  • The fight’s two branching prompts (shelves; chair or throw a Traci) are the options at the two rolls; where each falls in the fight is inferred.
  • The magazines come before the warehouse search, so reading costs no time.
  • SHOOT is listed before SPARE on both prompts, in the template’s order; option IDs retain their earlier meanings.
  • The timed prompts’ no-input outcomes are not offered.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether the second android can be missed in the interview (the wiki says yes; the template’s follow-up is unconditional).
  • Which answers give AGGRESSIVE vs CALM APPROACH, and which node EXPLAIN gives.
  • Whether the shutdown can cut the interview short before the fourth prompt.
  • The memory chain: how many hops, how many wrong probes fit before the reset, and whether Hank pays for every rental.
  • Whether any prompt appears at the memory reset, and whether Hank’s CONTINUE / GIVE UP can appear during the chain.
  • Whether the corner Tracis need the blue blood first, and whether the warehouse LEAVE prompt can be declined.
  • Where the second blue-blood serial (#354 411 057) is scanned.
  • What no input at SHOOT / SPARE does to the arrows.
