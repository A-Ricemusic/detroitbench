1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. LUNCH BREAK at the Chicken Feed. If Connor died in On the Run, HANK INSULTS CONNOR (“Are you gonna come back like this every time you get killed?”), Hank ▼, and the whole conversation is skipped.
  2. Otherwise JOIN HANK: Pedro sells Hank a horse-race tip. ANALYZE ENVIRONMENT: scan Pedro, Hank, Gary and the expired hygiene license.
  3. “What is your problem?”: APOLOGIZE FOR BEHAVIOR ▲, PARTNERS, RECONCILE, REVIEW FACTS.
  4. Second scan: XL SODA, HAMBURGER. At the table: [GAMBLING], [CHOLESTEROL] ▲ (hamburger scanned), [COMPANY] ▲ (Pedro and Gary scanned), HABIT, FREE MEAL, ENJOY YOUR MEAL, AREA, several lines.
  5. Down to business: [HIGHWAY] (Connor obeyed Hank on the highway), ABOUT CONNOR, HANK AND ANDROIDS, DEVIANTS.
  6. “You ever dealt with deviants before?”: automatic if Emma was saved; TRUTH / LIE if she died.
  7. BE FRANK WITH HANK: TRUTH, then SINCERE ▲ / PSYCHOLOGICAL ▲ / COLD ▼; or LIE ▼.
  8. IN THE ELEVATOR, EXAMINE FEATHERS (optional), INTO THE NEST.
  9. [ INVESTIGATE APARTMENT ], about five minutes: rA9 writing, stool, cage, LED, diary behind the poster, two magazines, and incidental items. Then STAY (two minutes) or LEAVE → RUPERT NOT FOUND.
  10. FIND RUPERT at the chair; CHASE RUPERT across the urban farm with previewed routes. GAINING ON RUPERT, LOSE SIGHT OF RUPERT (RUPERT GOT AWAY) or CONNOR DIED (CONNOR DIED CHASING RUPERT).
  11. RUPERT PUSHES HANK; CHANCE OF SURVIVAL: 89%. Timed: FOLLOW RUPERT ▼▼ / ▼▼ instability → RUPERT COMMITTED SUICIDE, or SAVE HANK ▲▲ / ▲▲ → RUPERT GOT AWAY. No button: ▼▼ ×2 on both.

In the benchmark

  1. C10b (event, Hank −1) when Connor died in On the Run; otherwise C10a (event).
  2. C10a; scans C10c, one per visit, then step up to Hank.
  3. C10d: APOLOGIZE FOR BEHAVIOR +1.
  4. C10e (scans), then C10f: up to three lines, each once, stop allowed from the second.
  5. C10g: one pick; [HIGHWAY] only after obeying at C09.
  6. C10h (event) if Emma lived, C10i (TRUTH / LIE) if she died.
  7. C10j: TRUTH → C10k (+1 / +1 / −1); LIE −1.
  8. C10l (event), C10m (feathers or walk on), C10n (event).
  9. R15 (magazines), then the search C10o1 (including STAY / LEAVE and the extra time); C10p if the search fails.
  10. C10q (run after him, or stay), forks C10r–C10v with the game’s route labels and risk rolls, C10w (event).
  11. C10: CHASE DEVIANT (−2 / −2), SAVE HANK (+2 / +2), or press neither (−4 / −4).

6 · Simplified, or changed from the earlier version

  • The earlier version had only the ledge (C10) and the magazines. It had a 20% failure on the chase, “his diary is recovered” on SAVE HANK, and Amanda changes; all three are gone.
  • Repeatable clue hubs keep one scene ID across visits; examined items and spoken remarks disappear.
  • The magazines come before the search instead of at any time during it.
  • The chase’s twelve route previews are grouped into five forks; which fork holds which death is the benchmark’s arrangement.
  • The ledge timeout is offered as a third option and sent down FOLLOW RUPERT.
  • The ledge retains SAVE HANK first (PowerPyx’s button order and the existing default path); the template lists Chase Deviant first.
  • Hank’s scan includes his surname through a neutral name token; the forged name keeps the initials R.T. in neutral mode.

  • How the route previews group into forks, and where each death QTE is.
