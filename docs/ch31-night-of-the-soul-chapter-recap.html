Night of the Soul: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s two flowcharts (PowerPyx 100% screenshots: 41 Markus nodes, 17 Connor nodes), the prompts, lines and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the two detroitbecometext templates resolved against the English string table), and the conditions from the Fandom wiki and PowerPyx. The chapter has no published probability, timer, public-opinion or instability value. There are no operator notes for it yet.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. Markus and Carl. Carl died in Broken: PAY RESPECTS at the grave (04:17 PM), four rounds of CONFIDE IN CARL, then Markus passes Leo on the way out. Carl alive: WELCOME HOME (07:31 PM), ENTER THE HOUSE, [EXPLORE THE HOUSE], GO UPSTAIRS, the caretaker, the bedside rounds, CARL DIES only after ANGER twice, LEAVE.
  2. Connor. THIN ICE → THE LAST ASSIGNMENT if Connor died in Crossroads. HANK’S HOUSE if Hank quit the force: three rounds, Hank’s speech about Cole or against androids, LEAVE, HANK COMMITTED SUICIDE.
  3. The people. Markus leads: MARKUS AMONG THE PEOPLE (no raid) or MARKUS ON SACRED GROUND → DEVIANTS REGROUP talks → DEMONSTRATION / REVOLUTION. Markus gone: North leads and calls the survivors to arms; with a deviant Connor in the church, the Connor chart’s CONNOR ON SACRED GROUND plays.

In the benchmark

  1. M26g grave (event), M26g1–M26g4 the four rounds. Or M26h (event), M26x explore hub (repeatable), M26u, M26v (events), M26b, M26a, M26c (rounds), M26d CARL DIES (event), M26e LEAVE (event).
  2. CN01 Thin Ice (event). CN02 Hank’s house (event), CN03, CN04 rounds, CN05 or CN06 Hank’s speech (events), CN07 round, CN08 LEAVE and the suicide (event).
  3. M26j or M26p (events), M26r talks hub (repeatable) with M26 TRUST / DON’T TRUST as its interrupt, M27 the speech. Markus gone: PN01 or PN02, then PN03 (observer events); or CN09, CN10 church hub (repeatable), CN11.

No risk anywhere. Events add what the character saw to his knowledge; the observer events carry no facts.

4 · Inputs and outputs

Read from earlier chapters

  • carl (Broken): grave or house. painterActivity (The Painter): piano, book or chess.
  • markus, markusActive: who leads. raid: church or Jericho’s hold. northLover: “I love you” and the shared moment.
  • Expected from Crossroads, with pre-rebuild fallbacks: ch30MarkusReturned (M22 option 0), ch30NorthWounded (M25 option 0), ch30ConnorDied (C22/C23), ch30MarkusDied, ch30JerichoBlown.
  • deviant, connor; hankResigned (Last Chance, Connor); connorDeaths; knowsCole (Russian Roulette); chase (On the Run); Kara and Alice’s state.

Set for later chapters

  • movement: demonstration or revolution (always revolution when North leads).
  • carl and ch31CarlFate (grave, confided, died).
  • hank false, ch31HankEnding and ch31ConnorEnding after Hank’s suicide; ch31HankTalk.
  • ch31ConnorFate (spared, killed), ch31ThinIce, ch31SharedMoment, ch31MarkusEnding, ch31ConnorEnding (Thin Ice, Hank committed suicide, or North decided to attack the camp; later endings replace earlier checkpoints).

5 · Where the benchmark adapts

  • Order: Carl, then Connor’s Thin Ice and Hank’s House, then the people (the wiki’s “leads to” lines).
  • Each topic menu is one pick, as the template records it.
  • The North-led scenes have no protagonist in them, so they are observer events.
  • Hank’s House is gated on the resignation alone. Until Crossroads stops narrating Hank’s suicide in C22, the house is skipped on paths where that line already ran.
  • Hank’s speech uses the template’s death count over the whole game; exactly one death plays neither speech.
  • The explore actions other than Leo’s message have no game text and are narrated plainly. The off-chart observations and READ MAGAZINE are not built.
  • REVOLUTION keeps the old markusOutcome = liberated that Battle for Detroit still reads.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Hank’s speech with exactly one Connor death: rails, confides, or neither (the chart’s direct TALK TO HANK → LEAVE line)?
  • Does DON’T TRUST move Jericho (the wiki says small down; the template shows nothing)?
  • Can several topics be asked in North’s and Simon’s rounds, or only one?
  • Thin Ice: only after Connor died in Crossroads, or also for a machine Connor who killed Markus?
  • Does an ousted Markus who left still get the Carl scene?
  • Does Hank’s House play, and Hank die, if Connor was deactivated in Last Chance or never reached Jericho?
  • Is LOOK AT YOUR PAINTING always available on the house branch, and what does Markus see?
  • CHECK ON JOSH: does a Josh who survived some other way (Markus ousted) unlock it?
