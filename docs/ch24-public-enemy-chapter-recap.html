Public Enemy: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots: 70 nodes, three bracketed investigation phases, eight endings), the prompts, clues and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext chapter template resolved against the English string table), and numbers and context from PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki. The only number the game prints is the HUD readout HANK SURVIVAL PROBABILITY : 40%, which the benchmark shows as narration. Every risk value is the benchmark’s own. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. FALL IN THE ZEN GARDEN (checkpoint) → TALK TO AMANDA in the boat: four rounds (HANK / EDEN CLUB; SINCERE / DETERMINED / COLD / TROUBLED; TOO FAR / NO USE / TRUTH only if the Tracis were spared; CONFLICTED / CONFIDENT / INDIFFERENT).
  2. INSIDE STRATFORD TOWER (checkpoint): HANK CONFISCATES COIN, or “Why did you shoot me last night?” if Hank shot Connor in The Bridge. LISTEN TO BRIEFING (hold the button).
  3. [ INVESTIGATE CORRIDORS ]: guard next to phone, Simon’s blue blood, dead employee, CHECK CCTV, phone, armed guard, each only where the tower chapter left it.
  4. MEET PERKINS; MEET COP FROM HOSTAGE SITUATION if the officer was saved.
  5. [ INVESTIGATE BROADCAST ROOM ]: SWAT bullet holes and blood on the wall, cap, WATCH VIDEO, CHECK CAMERA, Simon’s body, blood on the console.
  6. Free roam: DECIDE TO LEAVE (YES / NO), GO TO ROOFTOP, or ENTER THE KITCHEN. Finding Simon or the kitchen deviant ends the chapter.
  7. Roof: [ INVESTIGATE ROOFTOP ] → FIND SIMON (only if he was left alive) → RUSH DEVIANT (QTEs: SHOT BY SIMON or REACH SIMON) or STAY IN COVER.
  8. Kitchen: two magazines; INTERROGATE ANDROIDS in four rounds (the third needs CHECK CAMERA); the last aimed at one android → DEVIANT NOT FOUND or FIND DEVIANT → DEVIANT ATTACKS → OUT OF TIME or REMOVE KNIFE → RECOVER BIOCOMPONENT → LOSE TOO MUCH TIME or CHASE DEVIANT → RUSH DEVIANT / PROTECT HANK / DRAW GUN.

In the benchmark

  1. C14z The garden (event; reads the inputs into ch24In), C14z1–C14z4 Amanda’s rounds (C14z3 only if the Tracis were spared).
  2. C14e Inside the tower (event; ch24Coin), C14f The briefing (LISTEN TO BRIEFING or not).
  3. C14g The corridors (repeatable hub, six clues, exit to Perkins).
  4. C14h Perkins (event), C14i The officer from the terrace (event, three variants).
  5. C14 The broadcast room (repeatable hub, nine clues, and the exits roof 0, kitchen 1, leave 2).
  6. C14y You wanna go? (YES / NO; NO returns to C14).
  7. C14r The roof (repeatable hub; FIND SIMON or back down), C14r2 back inside (returns to C14), C14b CHARGE DEVIANT (risk 0.3) / STAY IN COVER.
  8. R24 magazines, C15a, C15b rounds 1–2, C15c QUESTION ANDROIDS AGAIN (no camera; returns to C14), C15 round 3, C15d which android, C15e round 4, C15f the attack (event), C15g REMOVE KNIFE (0.2) / CALL (0.35), C15h CHASE DEVIANT (0.1) or stay, C16 ATTACK / SAVE HANK / TAKE GUN.
  9. Eight ending events: C14x1–C14x4, C15x1, C15x2, C16x1, C16x2.

Routing: ch24Phase says where Connor is; every scene after the broadcast room is gated on it. The three scenes that go back to the broadcast room carry returnTo: "C14". When the chapter ends the phase is over and the cursor runs forward to the ending event.

5 · What the benchmark adapts

  • The three free-roam areas are repeatable hubs; clues are described as Connor would see them, with the game’s scan and HUD strings as the outcome. The wounded android is not named to Connor.
  • Leaving is offered once the roof has been inspected (PowerPyx’s objectives). The game’s timer (“Taking too much time”) is not modelled. The roof is visited once.
  • The kitchen questions go to the group; only the last round picks an android (C15d). The deviant’s visual tell is described round by round. The reactions of the wrong android to VIOLENCE, BLUFF and TORTURE are the benchmark’s.
  • DEVIANT NOT FOUND ends the chapter with the leave lines (template), although the chart draws no line from it.
  • Risk rolls stand in for the quick-time sequences: CHARGE DEVIANT 0.3, REMOVE KNIFE 0.2, CALL 0.35, CHASE DEVIANT 0.1 (wrong way). None is sourced. The corridor choice has no roll and no 14-second timeout.
  • The SINCERE lock and the coin follow the template’s primary condition (Tracis spared; Hank did not shoot Connor), not PowerPyx’s Friend requirement.
  • Assignment of the two BULLET HOLES scans to the three spots, the destroyed android’s scan in the broadcast room, and the place of Markus’s blood on the roof are the benchmark’s.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Instability on SHOT BY SIMON and on SAVE HANK: the wiki says down, the template shows nothing.
  • Amanda on DETERMINED, COLD (wiki: up) and TOO FAR (wiki: down): the template shows nothing.
  • Does the coin need Hank at Friend? Does SINCERE need the Tracis spared or a Friend Hank?
  • When can Connor leave: after the roof, after a timer, or both? Can he still go to the roof after a failed interrogation?
  • Public opinion on ATTACK (one arrow or two) and on each cop death; anything on LOSE TOO MUCH TIME.
  • Where HANK SURVIVAL PROBABILITY : 40% appears, and whether it changes.
  • How the kitchen tell shows, and which android it is on your save; what an innocent android does under TORTURE.
  • Whether DRAW GUN also counts as “saved Hank at the Stratford Tower” for Last Chance.
