Waiting for Hank…: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots: 26 nodes, the bracketed phase [ WAIT FOR HANK ], two checkpoints, two endings); prompts, button labels, clues and stat arrows come from the game’s own chapter template and string table (detroitbecometext); the Fandom wiki and PowerPyx fill in the rest. No probability, timer or risk exists in this chapter. There are no operator notes for it yet; section 5 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 7 what to check in game. 1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. IN THE ZEN GARDEN (checkpoint, Nov 6th 2038, 09:56). FIND AMANDA on the island; optional FIND MAGIC STONE (archway, left) and CHECK THE GRAVES (right, only after a Connor death).
  2. TALK WITH AMANDA: her reaction to Partners and The Interrogation, then three questions (the first only if the deviant was found).
  3. ARRIVE AT POLICE STATION (checkpoint) → FIND LT. ANDERSON. Reception TV (Arctic news).
  4. [ WAIT FOR HANK ], free order: explore, Hank’s desk (12 clues), the call, Officer Chris, the cells, Carlos’ android (if saved; he self-destructs after the talk), Gavin in the break room and the TV, three magazines, Fowler’s office.
  5. Beside the block: HANK ARRIVES → HANK STORMS OUT.
  6. TALK TO HANK (two prompts) → desk topics from the spare desk → START WORKING → EXAMINE FILES → TALK ABOUT THE CASE → HANK IS MAD (collar grab).
  7. HANK GOT A LEAD (Kara alive) or HANK LEFT TO GET LUNCH (Kara died in Stormy Night).

In the benchmark

  1. C08a event (records the cross-chapter inputs) → C08c repeatable garden scene.
  2. C08d event (reaction and its arrow) → C08e (only if found), C08f, C08g.
  3. C08h event → R12a media (reception TV).
  4. C08i repeatable waiting scene; its Carlos option cuts to C08b → C08j and back; it ends by the break room (C08k, C08l, C08m) or by sitting down; then R12 media (magazines; the break-room TV only after the break room).
  5. C08n event → C08o (follow?) → C08p event → C08q (Fowler, if followed).
  6. C08r, C08s → C08t repeatable topics → C08u repeatable case files → C08v → C08w.
  7. C08x or C08y event, recorded in ch12Ending.

4 · Confirmed by the template and the wiki

  • Every button label and its order, every spoken line quoted in the outcomes, the twelve clues and the six case files are the template’s, resolved against the string table.
  • Amanda’s first question is asked only if the deviant was found (the template nests it); INTERVENTION needs the deviant saved by intervening, REGRETS and NEW CONNOR a death in The Interrogation, STATUETTE the statuette seen in Partners, LAST NIGHT the spilled drink (the template’s only condition).
  • The ending speaker is Chris (template and wiki); the Kara-dead conclusion is the generic “We could follow up on one of these cases”.
  • Not built, because the template marks them cut: THANKS, HANK’S SON, the photo of Cole, the end of SIMULATION’s line, Gavin’s anecdote, “Think I’ll have myself another…”, “And bring that plastic thing with ya!”, Amanda’s transfer-request line. Not built because the template does not place them: the nameplate, tissue and police-cases strings.

5 · Simplified, or changed from the earlier version

  • The waiting phase is one repeatable scene before Hank arrives. In the game the station can also be explored while Hank is in Fowler’s office; here the cell, the desk and the call come first, and the break room is the last stop before Hank arrives. The magazines come after the station.
  • The self-destruction of Carlos’ android is narrated in the outcome of the second cell prompt, not as its own scene. Skipping the visit leaves him “not visited”; the earlier version destroyed him either way.
  • Following Hank to Fowler’s office is a plain choice (not a game button). That the argument cannot be heard from the desk is the benchmark’s reading.
  • The case files can be read in any order and Connor can conclude at any time; whether the game requires every file is not documented.
  • The stone in the archway is not described by any source; the outcome says only that Connor examines it.
  • The earlier version’s two options (“Ask about him” +1 and “Keep it strictly about the case” −1) are gone: the game has no such buttons, and asking nothing costs Hank nothing here. The −1 that the earlier version gave a cold Connor is replaced by the game’s unavoidable −1 at HANK IS MAD.
  • Until Partners and The Interrogation are rebuilt, the engine still gives Amanda +1 when the deviant is found and +1 for a confession, and −1 Hank, −1 Amanda and −1 instability for any Connor death; those chapters should drop the Amanda and Hank changes, which the game applies here.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Is Hank’s −1 at HANK IS MAD applied on the answer, at the collar grab, or not at all (the template shows no icon)?
  • Can Carlos’ android, Gavin and the magazines be visited before Hank arrives, or only while he is in Fowler’s office? What triggers HANK ARRIVES?
  • If the cell visit is skipped, is there a body in the evidence room in Last Chance, Connor?
  • Does LAST NIGHT appear after a spilled drink when the deviant was found?
  • Does leaving the break room before the news avoid the opinion drop, and does it show up later instead?
  • Must every case file be opened before Connor’s conclusion plays?
  • What does the magic stone look like, and what happens when Connor touches it?
  • With a confession and a later death in The Interrogation, does Amanda give the death reaction (as built) or the confession one?
