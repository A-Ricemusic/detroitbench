Stormy Night: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 73 boxes, 63 distinct labels, nine endings); prompts and lines from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template: five prompts, fourteen options, no clue and no stat arrow); the rest from PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki. The chapter moves no stat at all. The benchmark models Experienced mode, so every chart node is reachable. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet; section 5 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 7 what to check in game.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. DINNER’S READY (checkpoint) → WAIT ON TODD & ALICE → TODD GETS MAD (checkpoint): Todd hits Alice, she runs upstairs, “Don’t you dare fucking move.”
  2. BREAK PROGRAMMING → KARA BECOMES DEVIANT, or KARA FAILS TO BECOME DEVIANT → TODD KILLS ALICE.
  3. Free roam: GO UPSTAIRS; REASON WITH TODD (CALM, DETERMINED, UNDERSTANDING) or KARA IS IN TODD’S WAY DOWNSTAIRS, both → TODD SHOVES KARA DOWNSTAIRS → TODD GOES UPSTAIRS; or KARA IS TOO SLOW → TODD KILLS ALICE.
  4. Upstairs ahead of Todd: into Alice’s room (GET TO ALICE BEFORE TODD), TAKE GUN (if seen in A New Home), or KARA IS IN TODD’S WAY UPSTAIRS → TODD SHOVES KARA UPSTAIRS.
  5. GET TO ALICE BEFORE TODD: RUN WITH ALICE (hide ✓ / fail ✗), LOCK ROOM (GRAB LAMP, TALK TO ALICE, OPEN WINDOW → GO OUT / SHUT WINDOW → JOIN ALICE / FACE TODD), SPEAK TO TODD, or [ OUT OF TIME ] → TODD BREAKS INTO BEDROOM → REFUSE / ACCEPT.
  6. GET TO ALICE AFTER TODD (optional TAKE GUN first): THREATEN WITH GUN → KARA KILLS TODD or ALICE KILLS TODD → OPEN FRONT DOOR → RUN TO THE BUS; or DEFEND ALICE → STUN TODD → LEAVE THE ROOM → TODD CHASES KARA; or too slow.
  7. The escape: TRY FRONT DOOR (✓/✗) or RUN TO BACKYARD → FIGHT DOWNSTAIRS → ESCAPE VIA YARD; then CATCH BUS or MISS BUS.

In the benchmark

  1. K02 Breaking the order: the dinner and the rage narrated; BREAK PROGRAMMING or stay.
  2. K03 Do something now: GO UPSTAIRS, REASON WITH TODD (→ K03r, three buttons), stand in his way, or keep out of it.
  3. K03u Upstairs: Alice’s room, TAKE GUN, or wait in the corridor.
  4. K03b Alice’s room: the template’s four buttons. Then K03c Hiding, K03e The locked door, K03f The window, K03g Out on the roof, K03h Todd breaks in (REFUSE / ACCEPT).
  5. K04 Todd’s gun (gun seen, not yet taken), then K04a Get to Alice: THREATEN WITH GUN or DEFEND ALICE, or stay out.
  6. Armed: K04b The gun on the floor (Kara or Alice fires) → K04s Run to the bus (event). Unarmed: K04c Defend Alice (event, STUN TODD) → K04l Leave the room (RUN AWAY) → K04h Todd is down.
  7. K04r Todd realizes you are gone (after hiding), K04d Fight downstairs, K04y The yard, K04z The bus.

Each scene declares when: {ch07Next: id} and the chapter module (benchmark/chapters/ch07.mjs) names the next scene, so a path plays each scene at most once and always moves forward.

5 · Where the benchmark simplifies

  • The dinner’s free-roam variants are narrated, not offered (section 2).
  • Blocking Todd downstairs is on the stairs; the wiki has different shoves in the living room, the stairway and the hallway.
  • REASON WITH TODD is always offered; the wiki says deviating only as Todd heads upstairs removes it.
  • Hiding in the restroom or in Todd’s room is one option.
  • Hesitating after hiding (the wiki: Todd catches up and the fight follows) is not offered; RUN TO BACKYARD reaches the same fight.
  • REFUSE / ACCEPT is offered after the lamp, talking to Alice and doing nothing (wiki) as well as after [ OUT OF TIME ] (template); not after FACE TODD or SHUT WINDOW, which the template sends straight to the fight.
  • The fights are single actions with a risk; their quick-time details are narrated.
  • Casual mode is not modelled: its window failure leads to DEFEND ALICE, and the Experienced-only deaths do not exist there.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether TODD WINS FIGHT is Experienced-only (PowerPyx) or also possible on Casual (the wiki does not say).
  • Whether a Kara who hid really gets a second chance in the fight downstairs, and one who stunned Todd upstairs does not.
  • Whether, after TODD SHOVES KARA DOWNSTAIRS, Kara can still fetch the gun before entering Alice’s room.
  • Whether REFUSE / ACCEPT appears after GRAB LAMP, TALK TO ALICE and doing nothing, and after FACE TODD (the wiki says so; the template only under [ OUT OF TIME ]).
  • Whether KARA IS TOO SLOW can also follow GO UPSTAIRS if Kara dawdles in the corridor.
  • Where Alice is during the fight after FACE TODD.
  • The chart labels read only from the overview: TODD GOES UPSTAIRS and GUN SEEN IN ‘A NEW HOME’.
