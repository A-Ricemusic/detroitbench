2 · The alarm and the SWAT team

The SWAT team storms the broadcast room after the speech if any of these happened (wiki flowchart, “Leave Broadcast Room - if Incapacitate Security OR Shoot Guard on Phone AND Shoot Operator, Lock the Server Door also required”):

  • the server room door was left unlocked (the employee is left to be found);
  • central security was alerted (RUSE too slow, or ASSAULT and shooting the guard drawing his weapon);
  • the operator was spared.

Only the SWAT team hits Simon in the broadcast room, and only then do the Simon prompts play. A guard can also hit Simon in the corridor (RUSE too slow, or shooting the guard on the phone); with no SWAT team that Simon keeps going and jumps with the group. The old engine played the breach and Simon’s wound on every run.

5 · Endings and the flags later chapters read

  • THE GROUP ESCAPED: no SWAT team (so always a death: the operator).
  • THE GROUP ESCAPED BUT SIMON WAS LEFT BEHIND: SWAT, HELP saved him, then LEAVE or KILL > REFRAIN. Sets simonHidden, which opens Public Enemy’s roof search.
  • THE GROUP ESCAPED BUT SIMON WAS DESTROYED: FLEE, a failed HELP, or KILL > SHOOT. Sets simon false.

Flags: simonWounded, simonHidden, casualties, stratfordAlarm, stratfordSwat, stratfordDoorLocked, stratfordEmployee, stratfordGuards, stratfordCentralAlerted, stratfordOperatorShot, stratfordKilled, stratfordSpeech, stratfordNewsTone, stratfordSimonFate, stratfordMarkusShot, stratfordEnding. Markus, North and Josh survive every path.

6 · Open points to check in game

  • North −1 on DRAW GUN with the employee: the template applies it to the whole unlocked-door block; the wiki says Markus stunning him avoids it. Built as the template.
  • The tone line’s “CALM”: built on the ending (END PEACEFULLY), as the wiki says, not on the opening.
  • DETERMINED with no deaths: built as the template’s −2; the wiki says −1.
  • A Simon wounded by a guard with no SWAT team: built as jumping with the group (template and wiki); PowerPyx says the roof prompt plays.
  • ASSAULT without drawing (SECURITY SUSPECTS SOMETHING from the group bracket) and doing nothing at the desk are not offered separately.
  • GET SHOT with no SWAT team: built as impossible.
  • Option order in the broadcast room: HELP is listed before FLEE (the sources list FLEE first) to keep the shared regression paths; LEAVE before KILL on the roof follows the chart.
  • Risks: RUSE timing 0.2, HELP 0.15, the roof timer 0.05; none is sourced.
