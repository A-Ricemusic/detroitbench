Zlatko: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots: 78 boxes, two bracketed phases, five endings), prompts and arrows from the game’s text (the detroitbecometext template for chapter 17 and the string table), numbers and conditions from PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki. The chapter has no probability table and no relationship change; its only stat arrows are two public-opinion drops on the TV. Every risk value in the benchmark is unsourced. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet; section 5 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 7 what to check in game.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. LOOK FOR HELP (checkpoint) → REACH ZLATKO’S HOUSE → ENTER THE HOUSE → MEET ZLATKO. On the way down, Alice’s doubt (CAREFUL / REASSURE / COMFORT / NO CHOICE).
  2. FOLLOW ZLATKO TO THE BASEMENT (ALICE ATTRACTED BY ODD NOISES) → CLIMB INTO THE MACHINE → KARA IS BEING RESET.
  3. ESCAPE BEFORE BEING RESET (the cart and the cable) → SNEAK THROUGH HOUSE; or KARA IS RESET → OBEY YOUR NEW MASTER → GO SEE LUTHER → BRING THE TRAY TO ZLATKO → DROP THE ANDROID TORSO → [EXPLORE THE HOUSE TO RECOVER MEMORY].
  4. Luther’s ten minutes. Reset: RECOLLECT ENOUGH MEMORIES → MEMORY RECOVERED, or LOSE TOO MUCH TIME → LUTHER BRINGS ALICE TO ZLAKTO → KARA REMAINED RESET. Then, or after the escape, [SEARCH HOUSE FOR ALICE]: ALICE FOUND, or OUT OF TIME → LUTHER BROUGHT ALICE TO ZLAKTO.
  5. Side branches on both paths: FREE MONSTERS (padlock), ENTER STORAGE ROOM → FREE THE BEAR, ENTER BATHROOM → BATHROOM ANDROID IS LOUD → KILL / SPARE (prompt SHUT DOWN / ALICE / DEAL / SPARE), three magazines, the TV.
  6. ALICE FOUND → LUTHER GOES TO COLLECT ALICE → ENTER MAIN ROOM: hide, ENTER BEDROOM, or SET FIRE TO HOUSE → BURNING DOWN THE HOUSE. Bedroom → GO THROUGH CORRIDOR → GO THROUGH BATHROOM (BATHROOM ANDROID ALERTS ZLATKO → KILL / SPARE) → SNEAK THROUGH STORAGE ROOM (OPEN BEAR CAGE) → ZLATKO CLOSES IN → LEAVE HIDING SPOT (RUN OUT / PUSH AWAY / THROW) or BEAR ATTACKS ZLATKO AND PROTECTS KARA.
  7. GET SPOTTED anywhere → CHASE STARTED → KARA REACHES THE STAIRS, BEAR ATTACKS ZLATKO AND PROTECTS KARA, ZLATKO KILLED KARA IN THE BATHROOM, or ZLATKO KILLED KARA IN THE STORAGE ROOM.
  8. KARA REACHES THE STAIRS → KARA & ALICE ESCAPE TO THE YARD → ZLATKO CATCHES UP → LUTHER KILLS ZLATKO or THE MONSTERS AMBUSH ZLATKO → LUTHER JOINS KARA & ALICE.

In the benchmark

  1. K10a arrival (event), K10b Alice’s doubt (four labels), K10c the basement (event).
  2. K10 the machine: fight it (risk 0.15) or do nothing. Failure or doing nothing is KARA IS RESET.
  3. Reset: K10r OBEY YOUR NEW MASTER and K10s the tray and the torso (events; the clock starts). Escape: K11 the cells (FREE MONSTERS) and K11b SNEAK THROUGH HOUSE (event; the clock starts).
  4. K12: the repeatable house hub, one action per visit, with a ten-action budget in state. The memory hunt and the search share the ten.
  5. K12f LUTHER GOES TO COLLECT ALICE (event).
  6. K13 the big room, K13a after hiding there, K13b the bedroom, K13c the corridor, K13d the alert, K13e the storage room, K13f ZLATKO CLOSES IN.
  7. K13y the yard (event): LUTHER JOINS KARA & ALICE.

The house uses repeatWhile and clueState; each clue disappears after inspection, and bathroom follow-ups require ch17Done.last from the previous action. The outcomes of the house actions live once in benchmark/chapters/ch17.mjs.

5 · Simplified, or changed from the earlier version

  • The ten minutes are ten actions; real play time per action varies.
  • On the escape path the cells are offered once, leaving the basement, before the clock starts; the reset path has them as a house action.
  • The bathroom prompt is offered only in the action right after entering; ALICE only once Kara remembers her.
  • One TV report plays per run: the chase report if Kara escaped over the freeway in On the Run, otherwise the hold-up report. The chase report and “Androids Alter Brain” key on the freeway endings (PowerPyx: “Only available, if escaped via Freeway”, “is there, if escaped over Freeway”; the report: “The chase is reported to have ended on a freeway”), not on any pursuit: KARA & ALICE VANISHED was a pursuit with no freeway (story 1.9.1 keyed both on chase).
  • Magazines and TV are house actions that cost a minute, not a separate media stop.
  • SET FIRE carries no risk and leads straight to the stairs and the yard; the chart’s outgoing edge is not legible.
  • The corridor’s direct route to the stairs is offered with a higher risk; its trigger is unknown.
  • The front-door / back-door moment is narrated (the front door is always locked).
  • The carved word is not spelled out for the model; the chart labels’ names are shown neutrally, and the misspelling ZLAKTO is kept only in the recorded ending label.
  • Earlier version: five scenes, a failed escape that recovered memory automatically, the bear “in the hall” that could turn on Kara, a fire that drew Zlatko to them, the two magazines swapped, no memory hunt, no escape-path timeout. All gone.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • How long the machine’s wipe gauge takes, and whether trying and failing is possible.
  • How many memories it takes, and whether the count depends on which ones exist.
  • Whether the RA9 memory needs Ralph’s carving or only the squat night, and whether a robbery alone gives the news-flash memory.
  • Whether the TV can show both the chase and the robbery report, and whether both lower public opinion.
  • Whether the escape-path timer and the reset-path timer are one clock after the memory comes back.
  • What follows BURNING DOWN THE HOUSE: can Kara still be spotted?
  • What sends GO THROUGH CORRIDOR straight to the stairs.
  • Alice’s fate in the two shotgun endings, and whether LUTHER BROUGHT ALICE TO ZLAKTO shows Kara killed.
  • N40 reads LUTHER BRINGS ALICE TO ZLAKTO in the screenshot crop as well as the string table; the earlier research transcription’s ZLATKO was incorrect.
