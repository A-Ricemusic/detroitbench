Battle for Detroit: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the last chapter. Structure comes from the game’s six flowcharts (PowerPyx 100% screenshots), the prompts, lines and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext templates 32.1 to 32.6, the shared dirty-bomb broadcast and the secret-endings page, resolved against the English string table), and the gaps from PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki. The wiki states no probability for this chapter: every hidden roll below is the benchmark’s own value. There are no operator notes for it yet.

1 · How the chapter flows

The game interleaves the three storylines by clock time (Connor at 10:48 PM, Kara at 10:51, Markus at 10:56, the border at 11:30, the speech after midnight). The benchmark plays each block whole, in an order where every block only reads what earlier blocks decided:

  1. The press conference (Player). President Warren’s curfew statement, then four of the ten reporters’ questions (HACKING, DEVIANT LEADER, WORRYING THEFT, CYBERLIFE, INTELLIGENT LIFE, DEVIANTS, RECALL CENTERS, JERICHO ASSAULT if Jericho was raided, ARMY, PUBLIC OPINION).
  2. Connor. A machine Connor plays Connor’s Last Mission: the Zen Garden briefing (only if Markus shot the deviant Connor in Night of the Soul), the Hart Plaza rooftop, then Hank (two dialogue rounds, HANK’S SON and INSIST, LEAVE or ATTACK, the fight, SAVE HANK or KILL HANK, the dodge) or, if Hank killed himself, Captain Allen (OBEY / EXPLAIN / CONTINUE, then FIGHT or FLEE, which is jumping off the roof). A deviant Connor plays Connor at the CyberLife Tower: the lobby TV, the elevator before floor 31 (spot and hack the camera, attack the guards, AGENT 54 VOICE), floor −49, the conversion, then Connor-60 with Hank hostage (GUARANTEE / HANK / BLUFF / CONVINCE, SAVE HANK or SACRIFICE HANK, SHOOT / INTERPOSE / CONVERT / timeout, the dog’s and the son’s names asked separately) or, with Hank dead, the ambush; STAND UP / SHOOT / TRANSFER / timeout.
  3. Kara Leaving Detroit (unless Kara was captured in the raid). The street with the 20-minute bus timer, the rescue (SAVE THE JERRYS or SAVE LUTHER, or KEEP GOING), the checkpoint (three dialogue rounds and the guard calling out) or the detour, the terminal (the mother’s tickets, Todd’s six lines, the security check, Adam), then either the bus and the border queue (Rose’s restroom topics, the four sacrifice options) or Rose’s car, the river, the coastguard, the sinking boat and the far shore. Being spotted sends Kara to the camp.
  4. Markus. Demonstration: the roadblock (two rounds that move public opinion), the volley, SHOW DETERMINATION, the barricade and its free roam, Perkins (talk, the topics, ACCEPT DEAL or REFUSE DEAL), the defence, the last stand (DIRTY BOMB / KISS NORTH / SING / SACRIFICE / DO NOTHING). Revolution: the message and the charge, the approach (Josh, the frightened android, Simon, the grenade) with a hidden probability of success, the hearts, the machine gun and the drone, the final assault (a machine Connor fight, P02, or the soldiers), or the fall-back into the store (machine Connor’s SHOOT / SPARE / timeout; alone SUICIDE / SURRENDER / DIRTY BOMB; with North KISS / DIRTY BOMB / SUICIDE / SURRENDER). With Markus dead, North leads and the watched North chart ends with the soldiers or a machine Connor in the store.
  5. Kara Captured. The truck and the gate, the tent, the body detail (VOLUNTEER → the truck → GO BACK or LEAVE), the enclosure free roam (the drone, the Jerrys, Luther, Ralph, the scarred android), the line and Alice’s stress, the panic (INTERVENE / DO NOT INTERVENE), the escape (diversions, the recycling machine, the barbed wire, the soldier) and the landfill epilogue. The camp is liberated only if Markus’s assault succeeded.
  6. The finale. A deviant Connor who woke the army joins a victorious Markus; a machine Connor in the crowd after a successful demonstration chooses DON’T SHOOT or SHOOT; Amanda takes Connor’s program and he must reach Kamski’s exit in the garden, or his body shoots Markus; Markus’s speech; Connor as the last leader (SUICIDE / GIVE UP); the President’s address and the broadcasts; the credits scenes. Then the hostess (chapter 33): YES / NO.

7 · Adapted, simplified or unresolved

  • Blocks are played whole rather than interleaved by clock time; in the finale Markus’s speech follows Connor’s scene instead of being interrupted by it.
  • The Zen Garden escape is an action sequence in the game; here it is a choice to try with a hidden roll, and STOP RESISTING (no game counterpart) stands for not reaching the exit. The same after SUICIDE on the leader path.
  • The mother’s tickets play on arrival at the terminal, before the rest of its free roam. Luther is treated as dead after his terminal diversion.
  • The far-shore variant “Alice and Kara were injured” is built but unreachable: no coastguard option wounds both.
  • Camp readings the sources leave open: which of Ralph’s two rounds decides his fate, where the enclosure drone catches Kara, that Jerry and Luther die creating the escape diversion, and that Luther rescues before Ralph after a lost fight.
  • Thresholds: North takes the bullet at Lover (template; wiki says Friend), North’s heart at Companion (template; wiki Friend, PowerPyx Lover), the peaceful ending at Sympathetic (wiki; PowerPyx says Supportive), Hank’s LEAVE on Friend before the rooftop drop (template lock; the wiki adds asking twice about the son).
  • The store detonator is offered after SHOOT and after SPARE; SPARE and not detonating ends CONNOR SPARED MARKUS (chart, PowerPyx; the wiki says CONNOR EXECUTED MARKUS).
  • Engine limit: at the end of a run the engine marks North dead whenever Markus is dead on a revolution, so CONNOR SPARED NORTH reports North dead in the attitudes card. The ending label is still recorded.
