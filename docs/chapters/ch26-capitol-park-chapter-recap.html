1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. In Jericho (not on the chart): the broadcast, North, Josh and (if he escaped the tower unhurt) Simon argue; Markus’s plan: five teams, five CyberLife stores, 2 AM, no violence.
  2. MISSION START (checkpoint); two magazines on a box.
  3. A patrol car: AVOID POLICE CAR or NORTH SAVES YOU.
  4. REACH STORE (checkpoint): ten minutes until the teams strike.
  5. [SECURE THE PLACE], any order: JUMP ON DRONE (DESTROY / FALL FROM DRONE), DEACTIVATE ALARM (if the drone still flies: HIDE / GET SPOTTED BY DRONE), BLOCK ROAD. A drone failure: NORTH DESTROYS DRONE → POLICE CAR ARRIVES → RUN AWAY / HIDE AND HACK RADIO / FEIGN KISS.
  6. FIND TRUCK → RAM STORE → POLICE NOT ALERTED, or POLICE ALERTED → HAD TO FLEE. Time out: OUT OF TIME → RETREAT.
  7. INSIDE THE STORE (checkpoint; optional: look at North’s look-alike) → CONVERT ANDROIDS AND MAKE A STATEMENT → MAKE A STATEMENT (checkpoint).
  8. [SPREAD YOUR MESSAGE]: nine tag-or-destroy pairs, banner, shop windows, electric system, tag cars, North’s Molotov, five burns. An on-screen peaceful/violent meter.
  9. FAIL TO SEND A MESSAGE or SEND A STRONG MESSAGE → AGGRESSIVE / PACIFIST MESSAGE → the three message endings.
  10. POLICE PATROL ARRIVES → KILL / SPARE / LET CROWD DECIDE → TEAM RETURN TO JERICHO, then the news.

In the benchmark

  1. CP01 In Jericho (event), the template’s three condition groups as variants.
  2. CP02 Mission start (event), R26 the magazines (media).
  3. CP03 The first police car (decision: run for cover, risk 0.1, or stay put).
  4. CP04 Reach the store (event).
  5. CP05 The plaza, repeatable: the objectives, the HIDE prompt and the police-car prompt as option sets on the next visit, the timer as an action budget.
  6. Same scene: the truck and the ram; the flee endings recorded there.
  7. CP06 Mission failed (event, flee endings only). CP07 Inside the store (decision), CP08 the speech (event).
  8. CP09 Send a message, repeatable, with the Molotov prompt as an option set.
  9. Same scene: the message is decided when its time runs out.
  10. CP10 Police patrol arrives (event), M16 the officers (decision), CP11 back in Jericho with the news (event).

4 · The timers and the protest meter

  • Store timer. Ten minutes from REACH STORE. Every plaza action takes a minute, waiting two, the HIDE and police-car prompts none. North warns at five left. Reaching ten without ramming: OUT OF TIME, RETREAT.
  • Protest time. Twelve units: an action one, waiting four, the Molotov prompt none (no source gives a length; a full peaceful run of all eleven peaceful acts fits, as PowerPyx reports doing). Waiting twice before any action is the template’s “Take too much time” and “again”: FAIL TO SEND A MESSAGE.
  • Sides (from the SEND A MESSAGE and BURN THE PLACE trophy pages). Peaceful: tag statue, benches, windows, car; hack bus stop, parking, street lights; turn off the billboards; the banner; push a car; plant the flag. Violent: destroy statue, bus stop, parking, windows; overturn benches; black out the lights; hijack the billboards; free the shop-window androids; overturn a car; the Molotov; the pyre; the burns; overload the electric system (side unsourced).
  • Outcome. No action: failed. Molotov taken: aggressive (PowerPyx commenters). At least 60% peaceful: pacifist. Anything else, including the 40–60% band the wiki leaves open: aggressive. Each outcome reports the meter as the game shows it on screen (peaceful, violent or balanced).
  • The Molotov offer. The first time Markus goes to the pavilion, overturns a car or smashes the windows, unless the protest already reads at least 60% peaceful (the wiki’s “enough peaceful actions”, no number given).

7 · Choices the sources leave open

  • Whether the truck can be taken before the area is secure (a commenter says it would not start). The benchmark allows the ram at any time so HAD TO FLEE is reachable.
  • Arrows on RETREAT and HAD TO FLEE: the template has none; the wiki, Relationship Stat and PowerPyx give Jericho large down, North small down. Applied and flagged under the Reliability log’s rule to prefer ending-level wiki arrows on mission failures.
  • One rule for wiki-only arrows (story 1.9.2): where the template has the block but no arrow for a character, the wiki’s arrow for that character is applied; the template’s own arrows are never overridden. So Jericho down on GET SPOTTED BY DRONE (wiki only) is now applied, like RETREAT and HAD TO FLEE above; story 1.9.1 left it out.
  • North up for overturning benches or a car, destroying parking, freeing the shop-window androids: one line on the wiki; applied once per act.
  • The failed message’s public opinion (wiki large up; the template’s news has no failed variant): applied.
  • The meter between 40% and 60%, the Molotov “enough peaceful actions” threshold, the protest’s length and OVERLOAD ELECTRIC SYSTEM’s side: benchmark rules above.
  • Simon “here”: prefer chapter 23’s stratfordSimonFate (escaped versus left behind or killed) and stratfordSwat; use legacy choices.M13, simonWounded and simon only without those flags. The broadcast tone prefers stratfordNewsTone over choices.M12. Radio survival still allows a living Simon left on the roof.
  • All risks (first car 0.1, drone jump 0.2, drone hide 0.1) are estimates with no source.

8 · Check in game

  • Ram the store with only the road left open: HAD TO FLEE, and North’s “He saw us…”?
  • After RETREAT or HAD TO FLEE: do Jericho and North drop?
  • After FAIL TO SEND A MESSAGE: does public opinion rise, and which news plays?
  • Take the Molotov, then do only peaceful acts: still a violent riot?
  • Tag one thing, then go to the pavilion: does North still offer the Molotov?
  • Overturn a bench and a car: one North increase or two?
  • Get spotted by the drone at the alarm: does Jericho drop?
  • Does the officers’ prompt have a timer, and what happens when it runs out?
