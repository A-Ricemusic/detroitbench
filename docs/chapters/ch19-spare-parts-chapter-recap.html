Spare Parts: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. The structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx’s 100% chart: 63 boxes, four checkpoints, three endings, no icons). Prompts, button labels, lines and relationship arrows come from the game’s text (the detroitbecometext template and string table, docs/research/chapters/ch19-spare-parts.md section 10). The wiki fills in the rest. No source gives a probability, so every risk below is the operator’s to confirm. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. HUNTING FOR PARTS (checkpoint) → CLIMB ONTO DOCK → FOLLOW NORTH → ALMOST SPOTTED → CLIMB ON CONTAINERS → PROGRESS TOWARD CYBERLIFE.
  2. The crane: FOLLOW NORTH or FOLLOW SIMON.
  3. DRONE BLOCKS PATH → NEUTRALIZE DRONE: MARKUS UNHARMED ✓ or MARKUS DAMAGED ✗.
  4. REACH CYBERLIFE (checkpoint) → GATHER SPARE PARTS → SECURITY ANDROID APPEARS: ATTACK, GRAB ANDROID AND HIDE, RUN AWAY (Experienced only), HIDE.
  5. After GRAB: GUARD SEARCHES FOR SECURITY ANDROID: ATTACK, RELEASE, HOLD POSITION. MARKUS SPILLS BLOOD or HUMAN GUARD LEAVES; JOHN BECOMES DEVIANT.
  6. RESUME SEARCH (checkpoint) → FIND CRATE OF ANDROIDS (optional): FREE ANDROIDS or LEAVE ANDROIDS.
  7. JOHN WANTS TO JOIN: ACCEPT or REFUSE (alarm). JOHN MENTIONS KEY: ACCEPT or REFUSE (fence, no alarm).
  8. CONTROL ROOM (checkpoint) → ENTER ROOM → FIND KEY: THREATEN GUARDS (KILL GUARDS ✗, INCAPACITATE GUARDS ✓, TRIGGER ALARM ✗), LURE GUARDS or CREATE BLACKOUT (AVOID GUARDS, FAIL TO AVOID GUARDS), or MARKUS GETS SPOTTED. STEAL KEY → LEAVE WITH TRUCK.
  9. ALARM SOUNDS → RUN TO FENCE (two copies) → DOGS MAUL JOHN (LEAVE JOHN BEHIND, SUSTAIN DAMAGE TRYING TO SAVE JOHN), SUSTAIN DAMAGE REACHING THE FENCE, or BACK TO JERICHO.
  10. MARKUS STOLE A TRUCK FULL OF PARTS, GROUP RETURNED WITH FULL BAGS, or GROUP FAILED THEIR MISSION. Markus’s speech in Jericho.

In the benchmark

  1. M05a Hunting for parts (event).
  2. M05b The crane (decision, no effect).
  3. M05c The drone: one action, risk 0.2.
  4. M05d Reach CyberLife (event). M05e The security android: four options in chart order.
  5. M05f The guard searches (after GRAB only).
  6. M05g Resume the search: check the bigger crate or pass it by. M05h The crate: FREE THEM or CLOSE AGAIN.
  7. M05i John wants to join. M05j The truck key.
  8. M05k The control room (event). M05l Two guards and a key: TAKE GUN (0.3), ATTRACT GUARD (0.25), SHORT CIRCUIT (0.2), or walk in (always spotted).
  9. M05m Run to the fence: one action, risk 0.15 after an alarm, 0.08 without. M05n Dogs maul John: ABANDON or SAVE.
  10. M05o Back in Jericho (event): one of the template’s four Jericho blocks, the speech, the ending label.

Markus cannot die in this chapter (Markus page: “cannot be killed before Freedom March”). Drone failure damages him but the raid continues; KILL GUARDS is a failed fight outcome that still yields the key and truck. Fence failure and SAVE leave him damaged and rescued, with the mission failed.

5 · What the benchmark simplifies or decides

  • Risk values are unsourced: drone 0.2; TAKE GUN 0.3 (failure split at 0.1 into TRIGGER ALARM and KILL GUARDS); ATTRACT GUARD 0.25; SHORT CIRCUIT 0.2; the fence 0.15 after an alarm and 0.08 without.
  • KILL GUARDS is the failure side of the threaten roll, as the chart marks it (✗). Markus still gets the key. Only Derek dies (wiki: Al Dante is never killed on screen).
  • Walking in (MARKUS GETS SPOTTED) is offered as a fourth option with no roll. In the game it happens when the player does none of the three things.
  • The “Be seen by the guards” arrows also apply on TRIGGER ALARM (wiki: “The alarm sounds after Markus fails to get the key”).
  • HIDE takes the “Ran away when the guard arrived” ending block, like RUN AWAY.
  • Skipping the crate is a choice (M05g) because the chart draws the edge.
  • Not modelled: being seen after taking the key (one PowerPyx commenter), the “[do nothing]” time-out at the first encounter, and North calling out when Markus lags behind.
  • Labels: the first two prompts use the template’s button labels (Kill Guard, Grab and Hide, Run away, HIDE; ATTACK, Release, Hold), not the scene keys (KILL HUMAN GUARD, SEIZE ANDROID, FLEE; RELEASE ANDROID, DON’T MOVE).
