From the Dead: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 19 nodes); prompts, button labels, lines and readouts come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template, docs/research/chapters/ch11-from-the-dead.md section 10). The chapter has no probability, timer, failure node, relationship, public-opinion or instability change, and one ending. Markus cannot die here.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. BACK TO LIFE (checkpoint): NOV 6TH, 2038, AM 03:34. Markus reboots in the landfill after the police shot him (either Broken ending).
  2. ANALYZE SELF: four spots, all required, any order (unmarked on Experienced).
  3. REPAIR LEGS: two right legs (one incompatible) and a left leg; button prompts.
  4. HEAR ABOUT JERICHO: a dying android (Phileas) grabs him: “There’s a place where we can be free! Find Jericho! Find Jericho!..” and copies him the key to find it.
  5. PASSAGE OF LIMBS: hands grab him in a narrow chasm: “Where are you going?”
  6. JUNKYARD (checkpoint).
  7. [ SEARCH FOR PARTS ], in any order: FIND OPTICAL UNIT (the AK700 head) → CRANE DUMPS BODIES → DIG YOURSELF OUT; FIND AUDIO PROCESSOR; ANDROID BEGS TO DIE (optional) → REFUSE TO KILL or KILL; FIND PUMP REGULATOR IN ANDROID → REFUSE TO KILL FOR PUMP or KILL FOR PUMP; FIND PUMP REGULATOR IN DEAD ANDROID if she was not killed.
  8. CLIMB SLOPE with all three parts, then MARKUS CAME BACK FROM THE DEAD: he pulls out his LED and walks off: “My name is Markus.”

In the benchmark

  1. M03a Back to life (event).
  2. M03b Analyze self (event): the four Diagnose readouts.
  3. M03c Repair legs (event): the three leg readouts.
  4. M03d Hear about Jericho (event); sets fromDeadJerichoKey.
  5. M03e Passage of limbs (event).
  6. M03f Junkyard (event), with the template’s background lines.
  7. M03g Search for parts, repeated: each visit offers what has not been done yet. Going to the middle cuts into M03 (the android who begs to die) and M04 (the pump regulator), then returns. The slope needs all three parts and ends it.
  8. M04c Markus came back from the dead (event), every path.

No option carries a risk or a stat change. What Markus sees becomes facts in his knowledge, which every later Markus scene shows him.

5 · Simplified or different

  • The android who begs to die is optional and easy to miss in the game; here he is always met, right before the pump android, on the way to the middle of the junkyard. The wiki’s note that he must be met before replacing any part is not applied: PowerPyx’s own route meets him after the optical unit.
  • The dead android’s regulator is offered once the new eye can scan, as in the game, where Markus need never find the pump android (PowerPyx); going to the middle is optional. If he took it before meeting her, her prompt offers only SPARE. Story 1.9.1 offered it only after sparing her.
  • Both choices therefore come on every path. One interrupt per repeatable scene is what the engine offers, so the optical-unit chain (head, crane, dig) is one option rather than three scenes.
  • ANALYZE SELF and REPAIR LEGS are narrated events: every spot and leg is required and nothing can go wrong.
  • The AK700 head is the eye’s only source, as in the template; a PowerPyx commenter found a head on the ground instead of the wall.
  • No timer on the failing pump regulator: no source gives one (the previous version’s “you have minutes” is gone).
