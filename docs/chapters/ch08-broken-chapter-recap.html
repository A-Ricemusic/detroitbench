1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. AFTER PARTY (checkpoint): Carl and Markus come home; “No one gives a damn about art.”
  2. ENTER LIVING ROOM: wheel Carl in. Walking ahead alone plays “Are you planning on drinking alone, Markus?”
  3. POUR WHISKY: “Scotch, neat, as usual?” Carl sees the studio light; Markus calls the police (single button CALL).
  4. CHECK STUDIO WITHOUT CARL or CHECK STUDIO WITH CARL. Without him, Carl follows anyway (“Markus, no! I wanna come with you!”).
  5. CONFRONT LEO: Leo is taking paintings. Prompt REASON / ORDER / ADVISE / WARN; every answer gets the same reply. A timeout plays one extra line from Carl.
  6. Carl: “Markus... don’t defend yourself.” Leo pushes, slaps and taunts. BREAK MIND PALACE (button sequence, unmissable) → MARKUS BECOMES DEVIANT.
  7. Prompt ENDURE (chart: OBEY CARL) or PUSH LEO.
  8. ENDURE → CARL DIES OF A HEART ATTACK → POLICE ARRIVE → LEO ACCUSED MARKUS.
  9. PUSH LEO → LEO IS HURT → prompt EXPLAIN / BEG / CONJURE / SAY NOTHING (not on the chart) → POLICE ARRIVE → POLICE MISTOOK MARKUS FOR THE AGGRESSOR.

In the benchmark

  1. M02a After the party (event).
  2. M02b The living room (decision): wheel Carl in, or go ahead alone. Lines only.
  3. M02c Pouring the whisky (event), with the police call.
  4. M02d The light in the studio (decision): without Carl, or with him. Lines only.
  5. M02e Make Leo leave (decision): REASON, ORDER, ADVISE, WARN. Lines only.
  6. M02f Breaking the program (event): both chart nodes.
  7. M02 Leo (decision): ENDURE or PUSH LEO. Sets ch08Leo.
  8. M02g Carl’s heart (event, ENDURE only): carl = false.
  9. M02h The police arrive (event, ENDURE only): ch08Ending = Leo accused Markus.
  10. M02i “Get outta here!” (decision, PUSH LEO only): EXPLAIN, BEG, CONJURE, SAY NOTHING. Lines only.
  11. M02j The police arrive (event, PUSH LEO only): ch08Ending = Police mistook Markus for the aggressor.

No risk, requirement or stat change anywhere. Events add what Markus saw to his knowledge, which every later Markus scene shows him.

3 · Confirmed by the sources

Sources: chapter page, chapter template, string table, PowerPyx walkthrough, chart screenshots 1, 2, Carl Manfred, Leo Manfred, Markus.

  • Two endings (“This chapter has two different outcomes”), both cross-chapter impacts; the police shoot Markus on both, and he wakes in the landfill in From the Dead.
  • All 16 chart boxes and their order; the wiki’s text flowchart matches node for node.
  • Three prompts in the template, with ten options: REASON / ORDER / ADVISE / WARN, PUSH LEO / ENDURE, and EXPLAIN / BEG / CONJURE / SAY NOTHING. No stat arrow, clue or cut line.
  • No probability, relationship, public-opinion or software-instability change anywhere (the stat pages list nothing for Broken).
  • Deviancy is unmissable and comes before the last choice: both ENDURE and PUSH LEO are taken as a deviant.
  • Carl dies if and only if Markus endures. If Markus pushes, Leo appears dead but is hospitalized and survives (DEFEND YOURSELF trophy page; his voicemail in Night of the Soul).
  • Cross-chapter: “Push Leo: Markus visits Carl in his house in Night of the Soul; Read The Three Laws of Robotic Parenting in Waiting for Hank…”; “Obey Carl: Markus pays respects to Carl in Night of the Soul; Read Famous Painter Dies in Waiting for Hank…”.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether pressing nothing at ENDURE / PUSH LEO times out to ENDURE (a third route to Carl’s death).
  • The button positions of ENDURE and PUSH LEO on screen, to settle the option order.
  • Whether “Take too much time” in the talk-down skips the REASON / ORDER / ADVISE / WARN prompt or only adds Carl’s line.
  • Walking ahead into the living room: does Markus have to go back and wheel Carl, or does Carl follow?
  • Checking the studio without Carl: how Carl reaches the studio, and whether anything later refers to it.
  • What Markus sees in the mind palace (a red wall, memories of Carl?), so the narration can match.
  • Whether PowerPyx’s claim that “Carl also dies one way or the other” has any basis (the wiki says he survives PUSH LEO).
