Crossroads: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s three flowcharts (PowerPyx 100% screenshots: Kara 101 nodes, Markus 108, Connor 123, 30 ending cards), the prompts, lines and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext templates 30.1 Kara, 30.2 Connor, 30.3 Markus and the shared intro, resolved against the English string table), and the gaps from the Fandom wiki and PowerPyx. No source gives any probability or the deviancy threshold; every quick-time event and navigation failure is an unsourced risk roll. Researched in docs/research/chapters/ch30-crossroads.md; engine logic in benchmark/chapters/ch30.mjs.

1 · How the chapter flows

One chapter on three charts, intercut in the game’s order. The raid happens only if Connor located Jericho (Meet Kamski’s key or Last Chance, Connor); without it every character’s part ends quietly in the cabin and Connor never arrives.

Before the raid

  1. Kara: the drive with Rose (LOOK OUTSIDE, CHANGE RADIO STATION), the goodbye at the parking lot.
  2. Connor: AT FERNDALE, the painted hands to the ship.
  3. Kara: the hold, the news, a warm place for Alice by a fire; Luther tries to tell her something.
  4. Connor: EXPLORE JERICHO (Kara, Alice, the explosives, Traci, Rupert), STOPPED BY LUCY.
  5. Kara: MEET MARKUS (or MEET NORTH if Markus died in Freedom March), the android child, Luther’s (or Lucy’s) explanation, HUG / DISTANT. A distant Kara whose Alice was never above Distant sees her walk away.
  6. Markus: the counsel (tone; Josh’s accusation), MARKUS OUSTED if Jericho is Unpopular; otherwise the plan, the reflection, the detonator, the kiss if North is his lover.
  7. Connor: the captain’s cabin. Facing Markus, control alternates (Markus, Connor, Markus, Connor, Markus); facing North, Connor answers four rounds. Then BECOME A DEVIANT / REMAIN A MACHINE.

The raid

  1. Kara: RUN AWAY / FIND ALICE if Alice left; the corridor; the crossroad and Luther; the cabin door; the breach and the soldiers.
  2. Markus (leading): the team, the corridors (wounds), the androids, the timer, the well, Josh, the control room (machine Connor’s gun QTE, or soldiers), the escape, North shot, the jump. An ousted Markus chooses on a rooftop whether to go back.
  3. Machine Connor: pursuit, the cargo hall, then the gun QTE with Markus whenever Markus is alive in the hold, including an ousted Markus who went back (template: “Markus is alive - Connor finds Markus”; story 1.9.2), or, if Markus is dead or left Jericho, the corridor confrontation with North (always destroyed).
  4. Deviant Connor: with Markus alive he stays with North and can cover Markus and North at the exit (HELP MARKUS); with Markus gone he takes over the mission from where Markus fell (wiki list) and can arm the explosive and jump.

7 · Check in game

  • The deviancy threshold, and whether Markus’s three increases alone can tip a borderline Connor.
  • How many hits kill Markus (two, as the chart and template read, or three, as PowerPyx says), and whether Connor’s takeover inherits Markus’s wounds.
  • The Alice-leaving condition (never above Distant, or below Warm), and whether it needs the raid.
  • Whether KISS raises North (the wiki’s large up) and whether DON’T KISS drops Lover.
  • Josh’s sacrifice: relationship or only an unwounded Markus.
  • A failed save with no deviant Connor: North lives and Markus dies?
  • Settled from the chart (story 1.9.2): with Markus shot dead in the hold, a deviant Connor does not arm the bomb.
  • Whether HELP MARKUS is also played when Markus runs or cannot save North.
  • Whether the button order on screen matches the template or the chart rows.
