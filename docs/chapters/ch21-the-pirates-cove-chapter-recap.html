The Pirates’ Cove: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 48 boxes), the prompts, lines and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext chapter template resolved against the English string table), and the rest from PowerPyx, GamerGuides and the Fandom wiki. The chapter has one ending, no death and no probability. There are no operator notes for it yet.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. HIGHWAY TO HELP (checkpoint): Zlatko’s car in the snow, 01:02, November 7th.
  2. [ CAR ], optional: WATCH NEWS (three channels, plus a house-fire report if Zlatko’s house burned), TALK TO LUTHER (CAR, DESTINATION, DISTANCE, WEATHER), LOOK AT ALICE.
  3. CAR BREAKS DOWN → INVESTIGATE BREAKDOWN → SCAN ENVIRONMENT: “It’s 30°, Alice won’t make it!”
  4. INSIDE PIRATES’ COVE (checkpoint) → [ FUN FAIR ], optional: CHECK BUILDINGS, EXAMINE FROZEN ANDROID, SWITCH ON IVAN, LOOK AT MAP; a hidden timer has Luther find the tavern.
  5. CHECK ABANDONED TAVERN → ENTER TAVERN → INSIDE TAVERN (checkpoint).
  6. [ THE TAVERN ], optional: two magazine articles, LOOK AT RA9 WRITING, LOOK AT LEAFLET, LOOK THROUGH WINDOW, LOOK AT “PIRATE”; LUTHER STARTS A FIRE plays by itself.
  7. MAKE BED FOR ALICE → ALICE LOOKS AT POSTER → TALK TO ALICE (OPTIMISTIC / REALISTIC / SINCERE) → ALICE GOES TO BED.
  8. Optional OPEN BAG: OFFER COOKIE → ALICE IS NOT HUNGRY; SEE GUN → KARA NOTICES GUN.
  9. TELL ALICE A STORY: goodnight (REASSURE / CONSOLE / KISS / TOMORROW), then eight story picks.
  10. TALK TO LUTHER: topics [rA9] (needs the writing), [rA9 DOUBTS] (needs rA9), SNOW, PLANS, LUTHER’S PAST; then “Have you ever... Noticed anything? About Alice?..” (YES / WHAT? / NO).
  11. SHADOWS ATTACK → GRAB GUN (needs SEE GUN) / GRAB STICK FROM FIRE / PROTECT ALICE / PUT OUT FIRE → FIRE WARNING SHOT (needs GRAB GUN) / THREATEN (not selectable) / QUESTION / PUSH AWAY.
  12. ATTACKERS REVEAL THEMSELVES AS JERRYS → BACK OUTSIDE → GO TO CAROUSEL → SWITCH ON CAROUSEL → PUT ALICE ON RIDE → THE GROUP ENJOYED A QUIET MOMENT TOGETHER.

In the benchmark

  1. K14a Highway to help (event).
  2. K14b In the car (hub): the news, the fire report (only with fire; opinion −1), the four topics, the look at Alice, “let the drive go on”.
  3. K14c The car breaks down (event: three chart nodes).
  4. K14d Inside the park (event). K14e The fun fair (hub; exit to the tavern).
  5. K14f The tavern (event: two nodes and the checkpoint). R21 the magazine (media).
  6. K14h Luther starts a fire (event), then K14g: the tavern (hub; exit MAKE BED, Alice +1).
  7. K14i The poster (decision; Alice +1 on any answer).
  8. K14j The bag (hub): OFFER COOKIE, the gun (ch21SawGun), go to Alice.
  9. K14k Goodnight (KISS: Alice +1). K14l the eight story picks.
  10. K14m Talking to Luther (hub of topics). K14n his question (Luther +1 on any answer).
  11. K14o Shadows attack (decision; records ch21Reaction). K14p SHOOT IN THE AIR / QUESTION / PUSH BACK.
  12. K14q The Jerrys (event; ch21MetJerrys). K14r The carousel (event). K14z A quiet moment (event; ch21Ending).

No risk anywhere. Each hub is one repeatable scene: each visit offers what has not been done yet, and its exit ends the phase. The story hub advances through eight ordered prompts. Events add what Kara saw to her knowledge.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether turning on the news lowers public opinion (wiki) or only the fire report does (template).
  • Whether the story ending gives Alice a small up (wiki) or not (template).
  • Whether more than one car topic can be raised, and how many tavern topics come before Luther’s question.
  • Whether each of the four first reactions leads to the same second prompt, and whether PROTECT ALICE locks the gun out.
  • When the bag can be opened, and whether SEE GUN before the poster still unlocks GRAB GUN.
  • What LOOK AT ALICE, the map, the leaflet, the window and the pirate show.
  • Whether the boxes in the park buildings are YK400 or YK500.
  • Whether the large pale triangle beside LUTHER NEUTRAL on PowerPyx’s chart is a Luther down arrow.
