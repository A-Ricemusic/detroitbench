Freedom March: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure, node names and endings come from the PowerPyx 100% flowchart (66 boxes, seven endings); prompts, button labels, lines and stat arrows come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template and string table, docs/research/chapters/ch28-freedom-march.md section 10). The wiki fills in what the template does not show. Small arrow = 1, large = 2.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. TIME TO REFLECT on a roof near Jericho. ENJOY TIME ALONE: two magazines at your feet, LOOK AT BROKEN MIRROR, PLAY PIANO (leads into the beam), WALK ON BEAM (North arrives).
  2. NORTH ARRIVES: THINK / ALONE / DAYLIGHT; then “You seem preoccupied…”: FOLLOWERS, APPEAL OF POWER, REGRET POLICEMAN, FAILED (each locked by Capitol Park), NEXT STEP, LIE, SINCERE; then “The humans hate us…”: OPTIMISTIC, PESSIMISTIC, FATALISTIC, DETERMINED.
  3. “What was your life like before Jericho?”: TRUTH / SINCERE (SHARE PAST) or AVOID / NOT IMPORTANT (HIDE PAST).
  4. NORTH’S PAST or LEAVE. A hostile North refuses. Otherwise “I don’t wanna talk about it.”: OTHER NORTH (locked), TRUST, AGGRESSIVE, CURIOUS, UNDERSTANDING, or no answer.
  5. Friend or higher: her story, then the connect prompt (REACT / DON’T REACT): SHARE MEMORIES or NORTH LEAVES.
  6. If Simon was left on the tower roof and never found: REUNITED IN JERICHO, SIMON FORGIVES MARKUS.
  7. IN THE MALL: FREE YOUR PEOPLE (vendor, personal android, cleaner, security android), at least one before PREPARE THE MARCH.
  8. The street: personal android in front of a truck (its owner: CONVINCE / INTERVENE), parked androids, then the delivery androids block the road; OPEN MANHOLE.
  9. START THE MARCH: CONVERT AX400 or CONVERT ANDROID WORKER; CONVERT ANDROIDS, HACK SCREENS, CHANT along the way; bystanders shout by the Capitol Park outcome.
  10. POLICEMAN BLOCKS MARCH: RAISE HANDS / ADVANCE (chart IGNORE).
  11. REACH THE PLAZA, POLICE BLOCK THE MARCH: ATTACK / STAND GROUND / DISPERSE; ATTACK asks CONFIRM / ABANDON.
  12. After STAND GROUND or ABANDON: ATTACK / STAND GROUND / DISPERSE again. After DISPERSE: the police open fire; CHARGE / STAY / DISPERSE.
  13. Under fire (second STAND GROUND, or STAY): CHARGE / SACRIFICE / RUN AWAY.
  14. The fight (QTEs, with a Push for North): WIN (REVENGE / SPARE, POLICE RETREAT) or LOSE (SHOOT / DON’T SHOOT).
  15. SACRIFICE or DON’T SHOOT: John gives his life if he joined in Spare Parts; else Simon if alive with a good relationship; else Markus is killed.

In the benchmark

  1. R28 the magazines (media); M17a the roof, repeatable: the mirror once, the piano or the beam ends it.
  2. M17b, M17c, M17d: the three prompts with the game’s labels. The locked lines read chapter 26’s flags.
  3. M17e: North ±1.
  4. M17f (North ±1); M17g only after NORTH’S PAST with North not Hostile (checked after the +1). The initial response opens M17g2, offering UNDERSTANDING in place of that response. Each has its own arrow. “No answer” is offered as “Say nothing more and let it drop.”
  5. M17h only when North is Warm or Friend after the follow-up’s arrow (and not after AGGRESSIVE or no answer). REACT: North +2 and northLover.
  6. M17i event, gated on Simon left behind and alive.
  7. M17j event; M17k repeatable mall scene. Leaving with nobody converted plays Simon’s (or Josh’s) line and the scene goes on.
  8. M17l repeatable street scene with M17m (the owner) cutting in once; the delivery androids end it; M17n the manhole (event).
  9. M18a the first recruit (the bystanders’ shouts in the outcome); M18b repeatable march scene (up to three groups, the screens, a chant, keep marching); M18c the eight slogans, once.
  10. M18d RAISE HANDS / ADVANCE.
  11. M18e ATTACK / STAND GROUND / DISPERSE; M18f CONFIRM / ABANDON.
  12. M18g the second prompt (its ATTACK goes straight into the fight); M18h CHARGE / STAY / DISPERSE.
  13. M19a CHARGE / SACRIFICE / RUN AWAY.
  14. M19b the fight: PUSH (North +1) or keep fighting, both a risk roll of 0.35; M19c REVENGE / SPARE or M20a SHOOT / DON’T SHOOT.
  15. The same rescue order; Simon qualifies at Warm or above (his own regard).

4 · Where the benchmark chose between sources

  • Simon’s rescue threshold. Template “has a good relationship with Markus”; Simon page “at least Friend”; PowerPyx “positive (friendly)”; the chapter wiki Note “Markus is not unpopular” (the old version used Jericho’s regard); commenters disagree on Neutral. The benchmark uses Simon’s own regard at Warm or above.
  • “Friend or higher” for North’s story is the benchmark’s Warm level (its first above Neutral), read after the follow-up’s arrow.
  • No answer to North’s follow-up is offered as an option (“Say nothing more and let it drop.”), as is DON’T REACT for the connect timeout.
  • Two response stages. Video shows the initial answer followed by a menu offering UNDERSTANDING; UNDERSTANDING then leads to the story without another TRUST menu.
  • The second ATTACK goes straight into the fight; the template’s “See ATTACK” could mean CONFIRM / ABANDON again.
  • The fight is a risk roll of 0.35 (no published odds). PUSH is a choice inside it.
  • DON’T SHOOT carries no arrows (SACRIFICE’s sit on that option in the template), though Relationship_Stat lists the rescue endings under Jericho large increase.
  • Magazines: Android Riot after a successful store raid (wiki page), The Eastern Space Race after a freed store and an aggressive message (wiki page); PowerPyx saw both only after a violent riot.
  • Removed: the old Jericho +1 on standing, the Jericho and North downs on the first DISPERSE, North +2 / Josh −2 on a win, PO −2 on a loss, and the two broadcasts (they belong to Crossroads).

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether a Neutral Simon still saves Markus after SACRIFICE (benchmark: no; Warm or above).
  • At what North level she tells her story (benchmark: Warm, i.e. three small ups above Neutral).
  • Unobserved continuation and relationship conditions beyond the filmed initial-answer → UNDERSTANDING sequence remain unverified.
  • After STAND GROUND then ATTACK: is there a CONFIRM / ABANDON again?
  • Whether a rescued lost fight (DON’T SHOOT, John or Simon saves him) shows a Jericho arrow.
  • What REVENGE does, and whether INTERVENE (the owner) shows any stat.
  • Which Capitol Park outcomes show each magazine on the roof.
  • How many failed inputs lose the fight, and whether Push appears every time.
