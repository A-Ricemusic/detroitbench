Meet Kamski: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 33 nodes, two starts, two endings); prompts, labels, scan cards and stat arrows come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template for chapter 27, resolved against its string table); PowerPyx, the Fandom wiki and IGN fill in the rest. The chapter has no probability, timer or public-opinion change, and Connor cannot die in it. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet; section 5 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 7 what to check in game.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. Start A, if Connor died in Public Enemy: WAIT FOR HANK (checkpoint) → HANK ISN’T PLEASED TO SEE CONNOR. “Still immortal, huh Connor?” if he has died more than once, otherwise “You died in my arms…”.
  2. Start B, otherwise: ARRIVE WITH HANK (checkpoint) → GET OUT OF CAR → LEARN CHRIS IS DEAD or LEARN CHRIS SURVIVED, from Capitol Park.
  3. FOLLOW HANK TO KAMSKI’S PLACE: one prompt (INSTINCT, LEAD, KAMSKI; CHRIS only on start A).
  4. CHLOE ANSWERS DOOR → ENTER LOBBY → CHLOE GOES TO GET KAMSKI.
  5. [ WAIT FOR KAMSKI ]: TALK TO HANK (two rounds, automatic), ANALYZE PORTRAIT (the Amanda photo), ANALYZE PAINTING, ANALYZE SCULPTURE, and once the photo is analyzed READ “SPACE TOURISM”, READ “FORTUNE TELLER”.
  6. CHLOE RETURNS → MEET KAMSKI → QUESTION KAMSKI: three rounds.
  7. KAMSKI TEST: SHOOT CHLOE → KAMSKI AGREES TO ANSWER ONE QUESTION → ASK ABOUT VIRUS / RA9 (KAMSKI IS CRYPTIC) or ASK ABOUT JERICHO (KAMSKI OFFERS JERICHO KEY THROUGH CHLOE → DOWNLOAD JERICHO KEY); or SPARE CHLOE.
  8. “By the way… I always leave an emergency exit in my programs…” on both paths, then LEAVE KAMSKI’S HOUSE and one of the two endings.

In the benchmark

  1. C17a (event, start A) or C17b (event, start B).
  2. C17c: the walk, one pick.
  3. C17d (event): the door and the lobby.
  4. C17e, C17f: the two rounds with Hank.
  5. C17g: the lobby, repeatable; picking up the magazines opens R27 (media) and returns.
  6. C17h (event): the pool.
  7. C17i, C17j, C17k: the three rounds with Kamski.
  8. C17: the test. C18: the one question (shoot path).
  9. C18a or C18b (events): leaving, with the exit line and the ending.

4 · What the rebuild removed or corrected

  • Amanda +1 on shoot and −2 on spare, and +2 on the Jericho answer: no Amanda arrow in this chapter’s template. Amanda reacts at the start of Last Chance, Connor.
  • Hank +2 at the moment of sparing: moved to the leaving scene, where the template has it (same total).
  • “He will answer any question you ask”: one question. “Hand back the gun and refuse”: the game’s DON’T SHOOT.
  • The question had two options, one not in the game (“whether he created it”); now rA9, JERICHO LOCATION and VIRUS.
  • The emergency-exit line was only on the spare path; the template places it on both.
  • The magazines could always be read; now they need the photo, as PowerPyx and IGN say.

5 · Where the benchmark simplifies

  • Each start is one event; the walk prompt is one pick (whether several questions can be asked is undocumented).
  • The two rounds with Hank come before the lobby can be explored (the template’s order; PowerPyx says they happen automatically while waiting).
  • The wait ends when Connor chooses to wait for Chloe; in the game it is a fixed time.
  • The magazines open as their own scene right after they are picked up.
  • No source describes how the key passes “through Chloe”; the outcome says only that it does.
  • Whether Connor died in Public Enemy is read from ch24ConnorDied first, then the legacy flag publicEnemyConnorDied, otherwise from the current Public Enemy scenes. A Public Enemy death also costs Hank one step through the engine’s default death effect in this isolated branch, so on start A Hank currently drops two steps in all. Integration review: chapter 24 rebuild 0dc74b9 exports deathEffect={instability:-1}, with no default Hank loss. Once both rebuilds are merged, that duplicate death penalty disappears: the Meet Kamski arrival still applies exactly Hank −1. The shutdown ending’s explicit Hank −1 in chapter 24 is a separate template arrow and remains.
  • Chris’s fate reads chapter 26’s capitolChrisDead first, then capitolOfficers (spared / revenge / crowd), then legacy capitolCopsSpared and the original M16 choice.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether Kamski says the emergency-exit line after SHOOT too (template: both paths; Connor wiki page: sparing only).
  • Whether DEFENSIVE gives an instability up on “Whose side are you on?” as well as on “What do you really want?”.
  • Whether the magazines unlock after the photo (PowerPyx, IGN) or the painting (wiki magazine pages), and which chart node lights for the photo.
  • Whether the walk prompt allows more than one question before the door.
  • Whether the two rounds with Hank always play, and when during the wait.
  • Whether “Still immortal” counts deaths Hank did not see.
  • Who hands over the Jericho key, and how.
  • What happens if no button is pressed at the test or the question.
