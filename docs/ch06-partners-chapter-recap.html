1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. IN FRONT OF THE BAR (checkpoint) → ENTER JIMMY’S BAR → FACE SCAN CUSTOMERS (optional) → FACE SCAN LT. ANDERSON → TALK TO LT. ANDERSON: two of REASON, THREATEN, UNDERSTANDING, PERSIST.
  2. SPILL HIS DRINK, BUY HIM ANOTHER DRINK or WAIT OUTSIDE. Hank always comes.
  3. CONNOR AND HANK ARRIVE AT SCENE (checkpoint): PATIENT, FIRM or DIPLOMATIC; Connor follows Hank in anyway. Ben’s briefing.
  4. [ CRIME SCENE ], in any order, under a timer of about five minutes: the body and its reconstruction, the knife, the red ice, blood, the flyer, the closet, the writing, a blood sample, the backyard, the chair, the knife rack, the bat and its reconstruction, the shower (rA9) and the statue, two magazines.
  5. Ten pieces of evidence reviewed: CONNOR KNOWS WHAT HAPPENED. Time up first: HANK WANTS TO GO → ASK FOR MORE TIME → HANK REFUSES (spilled drink) → HANK ENDS INVESTIGATION, or HANK ACCEPTS → five more minutes → OUT OF TIME if they run out too.
  6. REPORT TO LT. ANDERSON: five questions. RECONSTRUCT THE CRIME, or FAIL TO RECONSTRUCT THE CRIME after three mistakes.
  7. FOLLOW BLUE BLOOD TO ATTIC → SEARCH ATTIC → FOUND DEVIANT. Every failure ends in CRIME UNSOLVED.

In the benchmark

  1. C05s Jimmy’s Bar (decision: go straight to Hank, or scan the patrons), C05b1 and C05b2 the two picks.
  2. C05a the drink.
  3. C05c the car, C05d the briefing (event).
  4. C05h the crime scene, repeated: one thing per visit; examined items disappear; the reconstructions need their clues; the statue needs the shower.
  5. Evidence 10 of 11: the report option appears and the clock stops. Clock out first: C05t cuts in, then C05x (back to the search for five more actions) or C05y; C05z if the extra actions run out.
  6. C05 the report, repeated per question.
  7. C05e, C05f, C05g (events), or C05u (event).

No option carries a risk. What Connor examines becomes facts in his knowledge; the flags in section 6 are what later chapters read.

4 · The clock and the evidence

  • The investigation is complete at ten of PowerPyx’s eleven evidence items: the victim’s reconstruction, knife (2), red ice (3), blood (4), blood (5), blood (6), chair (7), knife rack (8), the bat reconstruction, the flyer and the backyard. Each outcome shows REVIEW EVIDENCE n/10, as the game does.
  • Every look costs one action. 13 actions without completing the investigation is “about five minutes”: Hank wants to go. If he accepts, 5 more actions. The counts are the benchmark’s; the game only says five minutes.
  • The fastest complete search takes 11 actions (2 spare); the longest incomplete search is exactly 18 actions (9 non-evidence looks and 9 evidence), so OUT OF TIME is reachable.
  • Once Connor knows what happened the clock stops, and the remaining looks (the shower, the statue, the magazines) are free before reporting.
  • Cues: after 7 actions Hank opens the closet, tosses the flyer and comments on the red ice, and a technician mentions “something weird in the bathroom”; after 10 he wanders the kitchen and eyes the door.

5 · Simplified or chosen by the benchmark

  • The timer is an action budget (13 + 5); the evidence list is PowerPyx’s, ten of eleven.
  • The clock stops once the investigation is complete.
  • The two magazines are options in the search, not a separate media scene.
  • Tasting blood is one option; in the game any blood can be tasted once, and Hank only reacts if he sees it.
  • The report: a wrong answer repeats the question with all three buttons; the third mistake in all ends it; a wrong first answer is never a mistake. The game’s labels and lines are preserved, but button order is rotated deterministically per question and IDs are remapped to positions: correct positions 1, 3, 2, 1, 3. This deliberately departs from the template order to remove the always-first answer cue.
  • The patrons, Ben Collins, the bartender, the victim and the game’s terms (Eden Club, DPD, rA9, CyberLife) are substitutes for the subject; the lieutenant’s surname is not used.
  • Not modelled: the briefing heard from outside, the windows, the curtain of brooms, the bar’s bathroom.

7 · Check in game

  • Which ten items make REVIEW EVIDENCE 10/10: is the flyer one, the backyard, the bathroom?
  • Does the timer stop once the investigation is complete, or can Hank still want to go?
  • Roughly how many looks fit in the first five minutes, and in the extra five.
  • Is the wrong-answer count per question or for the whole report, and does a wrong button stay offered?
  • Does Hank refuse after THREATEN, PERSIST, FIRM and WAIT OUTSIDE without a spill (PowerPyx’s “at least Tense”)?
  • Is there any Hank arrow on FOUND DEVIANT (the chapter page says up; the template shows none)?
  • Can the statue be examined after REVIEW EVIDENCE 10/10, and does the deviant-found ending need it?
