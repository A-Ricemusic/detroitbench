1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. Only if Connor found the android in the attic in Partners (chart tag SUSPECT FOUND IN ‘PARTNERS’).
  2. OBSERVATION ROOM: Hank gets nothing; Gavin suggests roughing it up; Connor warns that deviants self-destruct under stress and takes over. Stress starts at 35%.
  3. In the room: REVIEW PHOTOS (the case file), the mirror, ANALYZE or INTERROGATE. ANALYZE is one action that records all five scans (PROCESSING LED, MODEL HK400, BURN MARKS, HIT MARKS, DRIED BLOOD) and shows the stress gauge, as the game’s single mind-palace view does; before story 1.9.6 the benchmark offered the five scans separately (operator, 2026-11-07).
  4. Round 1, two picks: FEAR, SHOW PHOTOS, WOUNDS, NAME. Round 2: COMFORT, REASSURE, THREATEN, BLAME. Round 3, two picks (or one if PROBE MEMORY): SYMPATHIZE, THREATEN, PROBE MEMORY, TRUST.
  5. “They’re gonna destroy me, aren’t they?” LIE / TRUTH. “Why couldn’t you just have left me there?” LIE / TRUTH. “I don’t wanna die.”
  6. CHOOSE APPROACH: PRESSURE IT, PROBE ITS MEMORY, CONVINCE IT. After a failure, another approach or GIVE UP; a second failure is an automatic give up.
  7. PRESSURE IT, then up to four PRESSURE / GIVE UP prompts. All four: he confesses, then self-destructs.
  8. PROBE ITS MEMORY: a button-mash QTE. Success: Carlos’s voice, “I know what happened”, optional APOLOGETIC / COLD, then self-destruction. Failure: RESIST TO PROBE.
  9. CONVINCE IT: three sets of three lines; confession only between 50% and 70%.
  10. ANDROID CONFESSES (Hank up), then questions: WRITING, STATUETTE, OFFERING, rA9, INSIST rA9 (the first three need Partners clues), ATTIC, TRIGGER?.
  11. Leaving the room: below 70% Chris grabs him (CHRIS INTERVENES); at 100% (probe, too high a confession, four PRESSUREs) he slams his head on the table (ANDROID STARTS SELF-DESTRUCTING).
  12. Self-destruction: INTERVENE (he shoots Connor and himself) or GIVE UP (he smashes his head).
  13. Chris’s grab: GIVE UP (he shoots himself, or Connor first) or INTERVENE three times (Gavin draws on Connor, Hank draws on Gavin, he goes back to his cell; “The truth is inside.” after a convinced confession).

In the benchmark

  1. Every scene is gated on suspect.
  2. C06o The observation room (event). Hidden ch09Stress = 35.
  3. C06a1…C06a8 Inside the interrogation room: one look per visit, one scene id per visit, until INTERROGATE. Scans become facts.
  4. C06b, C06c round 1 (the second without the first line; skipped if only NAME was there). C06d round 2, one pick. C06e, C06f round 3 (C06f skipped after PROBE MEMORY).
  5. C06g, C06h LIE / TRUTH.
  6. C06 Choose approach (no GIVE UP). C06r Choose another approach (failed approach removed, GIVE UP added). PROBE ITS MEMORY carries a 10% risk roll.
  7. C06p1…C06p4 and C06rp1…C06rp4: the four PRESSURE prompts, per approach round.
  8. C06qs Carlos’s memory (event), C06qr APOLOGETIC / COLD / say nothing.
  9. C06v1…C06v3 and C06rv1…C06rv3: the convince sets, per approach round.
  10. C06k The confession (event, Hank +1); C06m1…C06m7 one question per scene until none is left, then “I’m done.”
  11. Above 70% or after four PRESSUREs: C06sd (event) then C06s. Otherwise C07a (event) then C07, C07b, C07c.
  12. After the LED scan (which reads 35%, TOO LOW) each scene states the gauge band, and each line’s outcome gives the new value with its band (“His stress rises to 43% (TOO LOW).”), because the game’s LEVEL OF STRESS gauge shows a percentage (PowerPyx readers quote 63%, 73%, 93% and 16% read off it). Story 1.9.1 showed only the band and the direction; scenes marked stressReadout print the number, so older story snapshots replay as recorded.

4 · Choices the benchmark made

  • GIVE UP in the pressure branch: the wiki’s −20%; PowerPyx says −5%. It only matters if a later convince lands near the band edges.
  • Round 2 is one pick. No source says how many.
  • The pressure confession comes only after the fourth PRESSURE, which always ends in self-destruction (wiki: “all 4 times … will confess and then self-destruct”; PowerPyx readers: a pressured confession always self-destructs).
  • Shoot-Connor rule: GIVE UP on Chris’s grab kills Connor if he chose PRESSURE IT or failed the probe and got no confession (template: “Put deviant under stress and failed to get a confession”; wiki: “pressured him and/or failed to probe his memory”). A reader at 16% stress who chose an approach and gave up was shot, consistent with the flag rather than a stress value.
  • GIVE UP at the second or third Chris prompt behaves like the first.
  • Locks: FEAR needs the LED scan, WOUNDS the burn or hit marks (the template locks both on “Analyzed the deviant”).
  • Partners clues (writing, statuette, rA9 writings) are read from Partners’ state, which does not record them yet; until it does, WRITING, STATUETTE and rA9 are not offered.
  • “The truth is inside” sets suspectTrusts, and with the statuette examined in Partners also evidence.statueMap (the statue route in Last Chance, Connor).
  • No timer is modelled on the self-destruct or Chris prompts; none is published.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • GIVE UP after PRESSURE: does the gauge drop 20% or 5%?
  • How many picks round 2 (COMFORT, REASSURE, THREATEN, BLAME) takes.
  • Whether a pressured confession can come before the fourth PRESSURE, for example when stress is already high.
  • Whether GIVE UP is offered at the first CHOOSE APPROACH.
  • Failed probe, then a successful CONVINCE, then GIVE UP on Chris’s grab: does he shoot Connor (benchmark: no, there was a confession)?
  • INTERVENE once, then GIVE UP: who does he shoot?
  • Where the gauge turns from TOO HIGH to CRITICAL.
  • Whether FEAR and WOUNDS need particular scans or any analysis.
