Midnight Train: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 55 boxes); prompts, lines and arrows come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template and string table); the Fandom wiki and PowerPyx fill in the rest. The chapter has no probability anywhere. Its two arrow pairs are Alice and Luther small up, on KISS GOODNIGHT and on POLICEMAN FOOLED. The officer’s suspicion meter has no published values, so it is a hidden counter with an unsourced rule (section 4).

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. ARRIVE AT ROSE’S PLACE (checkpoint), Nov 8th, 17:10. MEET ROSE AND ADAM OUTSIDE; LUTHER AND ALICE JOIN KARA. Luther: “Do you think we can trust ’em?” (DISTRUST / TRUST).
  2. Optional CHECK ON ALICE outside (“I’m cold, Kara…”), then ENTER THE HOUSE.
  3. INSIDE THE HOUSE (checkpoint): FOLLOW ADAM TO THE GUEST ROOM, GET ALICE READY FOR BED. Three bedtime prompts; Rose’s spaghetti; SUGGEST EATING / KISS GOODNIGHT (Alice ↑, Luther ↑) / LEAVE. Optional DRAW CURTAINS. LUTHER WATCHES OVER ALICE.
  4. TALK TO ROSE: CAUTIOUS / SINCERE; WHY HELP?, MANY DEVIANTS?, DANGEROUS (after MANY DEVIANTS?), ROSE, THANKS; BORDER; INSIST / NO CHOICE.
  5. FOLLOW ROSE TO LAUNDRY ROOM: Mary has shut down. ALICE APPROACHES MARY: STAY (LET ALICE SEE MARY) or TAKE ALICE OUTSIDE (PREVENT ALICE SEEING MARY).
  6. LEAVE LAUNDRY ROOM; ROSE AND ADAM ARGUE; ROSE LEAVES TO FIND HELP (checkpoint).
  7. [EXPLORE THE HOUSE], all optional: TALK TO LUTHER (“Not now, Luther.”), CHECK ON ALICE by the fire (INSIST / LET IT GO; only after LET ALICE SEE MARY), CHECK ON DEVIANTS, READ MAGAZINE, WATCH NEWS, SEE FAMILY PHOTO. ADAM RETURNS; optional TALK TO ADAM TO LOWER TENSION (REASSURE / CONFESS / EXPLAIN).
  8. COP AT THE DOOR; ADAM PANICS. [FIND AND HIDE EVIDENCE OF DEVIANTS], about 50 seconds: CHANGE MAGAZINE COVER, HIDE BLUE BLOOD POUCHES, TELL ALICE & LUTHER TO HIDE (UPSTAIRS / LAUNDRY / KITCHEN; kitchen then DRAW CURTAINS or CURTAIN LEFT OPEN), SHUT THE DOOR OF LAUNDRY ROOM, HIDE THE ANDROID CLOTHES; REASSURED ADAM (REASSURE / THREATEN / CALM); OPEN THE DOOR or POLICEMAN INSISTS AND KARA HAS TO OPEN THE DOOR.
  9. [MANAGE THE POLICEMAN]: ALICE / NOBODY (LIE ABOUT ALICE), YES / NO, the blue blood (MESS / PREVIOUS OWNER / NOT MINE), MADE COFFEE, POLICEMAN SUSPICIOUS ABOUT UPSTAIRS, POLICEMAN DISCOVERS ALICE & LUTHER HIDDEN, POLICEMAN SEES ARTICLE, POLICEMAN SEES ANDROID CLOTHES, POLICEMAN QUESTIONS ADAM (TIRED / UPSET), the noise (DOG / WASHING MACHINE / WIND / HEARD NOTHING).
  10. Endings: LUTHER DIED KILLING POLICEMAN (kitchen, curtain open), LUTHER DIED SAVING OTHER DEVIANTS (POLICEMAN IS SUSPICIOUS, COP FINDS ANDROIDS IN THE LAUNDRY ROOM), POLICEMAN LEFT (POLICEMAN FOOLED; Alice ↑, Luther ↑). Rose: “Get yourselves ready. We leave tonight.”

3 · Confirmed by the sources

Sources: chapter page, template, PowerPyx, 100% chart, the officer’s page, Relationship Stat.

  • Three endings; two carry the cross-chapter-impact padlock; no skull or other icon anywhere on the chart.
  • Four arrows in the template, all small up: Alice and Luther on KISS GOODNIGHT, and Alice and Luther on “Policeman is fooled”. The wiki lists the same four. No public-opinion or instability change.
  • No probability on the chapter page. The hiding phase is “roughly 50 seconds” (PowerPyx; GamerGuides “a 50 second timer”).
  • The kitchen curtain left open is certain death for Luther and the officer; the officer hits his head on the table (officer’s page).
  • CHANGE MAGAZINE COVER needs READ MAGAZINE (PowerPyx); the advertisement raises suspicion if he sees it (Treat Yourself!).
  • CHECK ON ALICE by the fire needs LET ALICE SEE MARY (wiki flowchart list).
  • Kara and Alice survive every ending; Rose returns with “Get yourselves ready. We leave tonight.”

6 · What to check when you replay

  • With all evidence hidden, does DOG still send him into the laundry room? With nothing hidden, does WASHING MACHINE still get him to leave? What do WIND and HEARD NOTHING do?
  • Does the magazine lie open at the advertisement when Kara never reads it, and can she then change it?
  • Does leaving the laundry-room door open change anything? Does hiding Luther and Alice in the laundry room?
  • Does ALICE (rather than NOBODY) avoid the suspicion when they hide upstairs?
  • Does making coffee lower the meter, and does any Adam answer (THREATEN especially) change his stammer or the meter?
  • How many of Rose’s topics can be asked before BORDER, and in what order are they offered?
  • How many hiding actions fit in the 50 seconds for a deliberate player?
  • Whether the fireside CHECK ON ALICE appears after PREVENT ALICE SEEING MARY.
