Replay Partners (chapter 6) and read Hank's level after every arrow

About twenty minutes per run. Hank's gauge moves six to eight times in this chapter, and the numbers behind that gauge are the biggest unknown in the whole benchmark. Every Hank gate later in the game (whether he shoots Connor on the Bridge, whether the Friend endings open) runs on guesses right now. One careful replay calibrates all of it.

What to record

After each arrow popup, pause, open the flowchart, and write down the level printed in the header: HOSTILE, TENSE, NEUTRAL, WARM or FRIEND. The level after each step is what matters, not only the arrow. Write the readings in the boxes below (or dictate them in the playthrough journal thread).
