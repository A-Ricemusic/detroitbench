A New Home: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 30 nodes) and the Fandom wiki’s chapter page, transcript and Relationship Stat page. The chapter has no probability, public-opinion or instability change; its only number is Alice’s relationship, which the wiki gives as up and down icons. There are no operator notes for this chapter yet; section 4 lists where the benchmark simplifies, and section 6 what to check in game.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. WELCOME HOME (checkpoint) → JOIN TODD: “You’ve been gone for two weeks and the place is a mess.” Alice appears on the stairs. “Get started down here, then you do upstairs.”
  2. [ CLEAN FIRST FLOOR ], in any order: the magazine (READ “ANDROID POWER!”, READ “CONNECTING THE DOTS”), WASH DISHES, CHECK BACKYARD → TALK WITH ALICE, REWASH CLOTHES → FIND RED ICE → ENRAGE TODD, ACTIVATE VACUUM, DISTURB TODD. Not on the chart: the TOY line.
  3. TAKE OUT TRASH (mandatory; Todd then hunts for his Red Ice) → ASK TO GO UPSTAIRS.
  4. GO UPSTAIRS (checkpoint).
  5. [ CLEAN SECOND FLOOR ], in any order: CLEAN TODD’S ROOM (ALICE GOES TO HER ROOM) → TIDY PILLS → KARA SEES GUN, CLEAN TOILET, CLEAN BATHROOM, the magazine (READ “ANDROID QB”, READ “PUTTING A PRICE”) and the TV, CLEAN ALICE’S ROOM → VENTILATE ROOM → KARA DISCOVERS A WAY DOWN. Not on the chart: the Alice in Wonderland book, the locked box.
  6. TALK WITH ALICE in her room: four menus (the transcript: three lines, then four, then “Same Options But Down To Three”, then “Same Again But Two And A New Option” with LEAVE ALONE). She leaves; at Neutral or better ALICE GIVES KARA A KEY → OPEN ALICE’S BOX.
  7. FINISHED CLEANING: Todd rages at Alice, then apologizes. The only ending.

In the benchmark

  1. K01 Welcome home (event).
  2. R04 The magazine on the dining table (media): read either cover, both or neither.
  3. K01a The first floor, repeated: each visit offers what has not been done yet; going upstairs needs the trash. Talking to Alice in the yard cuts into K01y1 and K01y2 (two menus) and returns.
  4. K01c Going upstairs (event).
  5. K01d The second floor, repeated; talking to Alice needs both bedrooms and ends it.
  6. K01e, K01f, K01g, K01g2: the four menus in Alice’s room (LEAVE ALONE only in the fourth). The last one decides the key (ch04.mjs). Story 1.9.1 had three menus, with LEAVE ALONE in the third.
  7. K01k Alice’s key (event) and K01x Alice’s box (decision), only with the key.
  8. R04b Todd’s bedroom: the magazine and the TV (media).
  9. K01z Finished cleaning (event), every path.

No option carries a risk. What Kara sees becomes facts in her knowledge, which every later Kara scene shows her; the flags below are what later chapters read.

3 · Alice’s relationship, line by line

Alice starts at Distant (Relationship Stat page: “Distant (Initial)”). The wiki shows each change only as a small up or down icon; the benchmark counts each as one step. On its Alice scale (benchmark/scales.mjs) Distant runs up to 1, Neutral from 2, Warm from 4, Close from 6, Trusted from 8 and Family from 10; the operator saw one small increase still read Distant and eight read Trusted. The subject sees the level, never a number.

Most and least

  • Every increase: dishes, toy, GAME, BACKYARD, the book with Alice, FRIENDLY or DIPLOMATIC, NAME KARA, INTERESTS? = 8, Trusted.
  • Nothing optional, FACTUAL, SEEM QUIET, then NAME KARA, then LEAVE ALONE = 1, Distant: no key, no box. Once SEEM QUIET is said, the third menu has only lines with arrows, so no path through the four menus avoids one.
  • Same, but MOTHER? instead of NAME KARA in the third menu = −1, still Distant.

The key

  • Given when the bond after the fourth menu is at least 2, ALICE_KEY, the lowest value the scale calls Neutral (wiki: “if Alice has at least Neutral status”; “at Distant, Alice will not give Kara the key”).
  • Two increases are needed, so the dishes and NAME KARA in the room get the key; asking about her mother takes one back.

4 · Confirmed by the wiki

Sources: chapter page, transcript, Relationship Stat, PowerPyx walkthrough, chart screenshots 1 2 3 4 5. Full research: docs/research/ch04-a-new-home.md.

  • One ending (“This chapter has only one outcome”), FINISHED CLEANING, whatever else happens.
  • The trash and both bedrooms are mandatory (transcript: “Trash is mandatory”, “Cleaning both bedrooms is mandatory”); going up early or down early is blocked.
  • The relationship lines above, one decrease, and no probability, public-opinion or software-instability change.
  • Cross-chapter: “Kara Sees Gun / Gun accessible in Stormy Night and Fugitives”; “Kara Discovers a Way Down / Window accessible in Stormy Night”; “Open Alice’s Box / Allows you to persuade Todd if he’s alive in Battle for Detroit”; “Kara See’s ‘Alice in Wonderland’ / Helps Kara remember Alice in Zlatko”. Zlatko also recalls “Todd’s Red Ice Pipe (If Kara washed Todd’s clothes)”.
  • The box holds a clover, “a photo of Todd, his wife and his biological daughter”, and drawings of the mother, Todd and his wife, Kara being examined and Kara being broken. The Todd Williams page says the family photo unlocks persuading him.
  • The television facts: 225,000 jobs lost in October and 37.3% unemployment; the President’s 200,000 android combat units; one android player per basketball squad.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • How many small increases take Alice from Distant to Neutral, and whether one is enough for the key.
  • Whether every increase lands at Trusted or beyond (the wiki says Trusted is reachable “in this very chapter”).
  • Resolved by the game’s text (story 1.7.0): SEEM QUIET has no arrow; PowerPyx’s “Alice likes it” is not supported.
  • Whether the yard menus or the TOY line light the first-floor TALK WITH ALICE node, and whether TOY needs the first floor done.
  • Whether the room talk really needs the bathroom, the book and the shelf (PowerPyx) or only both bedrooms (transcript).
  • Whether Alice’s room can be finished with the window shut, and whether ALICE GOES TO HER ROOM can be missed.
  • Whether the girl in the box photo is visibly not Alice.
  • The legend for the triangle icons and for the grey DISTURB TODD box.
