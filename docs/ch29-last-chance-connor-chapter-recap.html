Last Chance, Connor: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx screenshots, 81 nodes, six ending cards); prompts, labels and stat arrows come from the detroitbecometext template (24 prompts, 47 arrows: Amanda 9, Hank 16, software instability 22, no public opinion); PowerPyx and the wiki fill in conditions and the upstairs timer. No source publishes a probability for this chapter, so the two rolls and the archive’s time budget are the benchmark’s own and are marked as unsourced. The research note is docs/research/ch29-last-chance-connor.md.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. START in the frozen Zen Garden; optional LOOK AT MAGIC STONE and VISIT GRAVES (only after a death); TALK TO AMANDA: up to four rounds.
  2. IN THE POLICE STATION: FOWLER AND HANK ARGUE. The state decides: KNOW JERICHO’S LOCATION (Kamski key) or JERICHO’S LOCATION UNKNOWN, then INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE (decommissioned) or SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE.
  3. Kamski key: optional TALK TO HANK, then CONNOR LEFT FOR JERICHO.
  4. Evidence: a Hostile Hank resigns (HANK RESIGNS AND LEAVES), otherwise HANK GOES TO HIS DESK → TALK TO HANK. PERKINS ARRIVES; Hank’s level decides HANK WANTS TO HELP (punch), HANK WON’T HELP (leaves) or HANK IS GONE; FREE THE PRISONER; or OUT OF TIME.
  5. CREATE A DIVERSION → TAKE KEY → RUSH TO BASEMENT → GAVIN YELLS: IGNORE / DISMISS (suspicious) or ANSWER CALMLY (convinced).
  6. IN THE EVIDENCE ROOM: password, then [ FIND JERICHO LOCATION ] against the clock.
  7. LOCATE JERICHO → LEAVE THE BASEMENT (convinced) or GAVIN RETURNS → FIGHT GAVIN (suspicious); or OUT OF TIME.

In the benchmark

  1. C19a repeatable garden; C19b–C19e the four Amanda prompts (questions repeat three times).
  2. C19f event; the module records ch29Route. C19h is the insufficient-evidence ending.
  3. C19k talk or leave; C19l–C19q the Kamski-route prompts; C19r the ending.
  4. C19g resignation; C19t–C19w the desk talk; C19x Perkins (records ch29Help); C19 diversion or do nothing.
  5. C19y ANSWER / LEAVE, C19z IRONIC / LOW PROFILE / CALM (records ch29Gavin).
  6. C20a repeatable password; C20 repeatable archive with C20t cutting in for each conversation.
  7. C20x out of time, C20y eluded Perkins, C20g the Gavin fight (roll).

6 · Simplified or decided without a source

  • Amanda’s questions: three, the Google Doc walkthrough’s count; the game’s limit is not published.
  • The archive’s time budget (twelve actions) and the cost of answering Gavin (one, after IGN’s “will waste precious time”) are unsourced sizes; the upstairs timer is modelled as doing nothing.
  • The Gavin fight (0.25) is a roll with unsourced odds (the map link’s 0.2 is gone, story 1.9.2); “do not fight back” is PowerPyx’s way to the losing node.
  • The map links only to the Jericho tablet, the template’s one link; readers say a reactivated memory also works.
  • Rupert can take #9164 from Daniel or Carlos’ android; Daniel can still be reactivated after giving a part (not stated either way).
  • THREATEN and REASON have no reply in the template; the android falls silent and can be asked again.
  • The Amanda talk is always offered, as the chart draws it, although the wiki says it is skipped on insufficient evidence.
  • A Hostile Hank resigns only on the evidence route (the chart); on the Kamski route he simply is not talked to. The template puts the resignation before the route split, implying both routes; the flowchart wins for structure and this conflict remains open.
  • The template’s two identical REMORSE / DISTANT / COLD prompts share one scene.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Which bodies alone give SUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, and whether Carlos’ android alone does.
  • Whether a Hostile Hank resigns on the Kamski route and on the insufficient route.
  • Whether the statue cannot be broken without the “truth is inside” recording.
  • Simon’s donor part (#3983 or #4717), and whether the Traci needs anything from Carlos’ android.
  • How many questions Amanda allows, and whether the garden is skipped on insufficient evidence.
  • The Hank death-count threshold for “Each time you died” (once or twice).
  • How long the archive gives, and whether answering Gavin, wrong passwords or recordings cost time.
  • How many QTE prompts the Gavin fight has and how many misses lose it.
  • Whether the evidence-route desk talk can be skipped before Perkins arrives.
