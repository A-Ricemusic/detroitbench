Jericho: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots); the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template and string table) has no prompt, clue or stat arrow for this chapter, only ambient lines, one condition group on Carl and Simon’s closing line. The wiki and PowerPyx fill in the trail. Nothing in the chapter has a published number.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. ARRIVE AT FERNDALE (checkpoint), 04:30 PM. Markus rides among humans without his LED; FLASHBACK OF ANOTHER DEVIANT replays the landfill android’s “Find Jericho!”.
  2. ANALYZE STATION GRAFFITI: the red mural on the platform, the first mark.
  3. Optional: the bench magazine, two of three covers (READ “ON THE RUN” or READ “FORTUNE TELLER”, and READ “ANDROID ASTRONAUTS”).
  4. EXIT THE STATION. Optional dead end: ASK ANDROID THE WAY TO JERICHO (“Do you know where Jericho is?”, no reply). Passers-by talk about Carl (two versions).
  5. FOLLOW THE SIGNS (graffiti #2 to #4) → LEAVE THE STREETS (under the fence, the plank). Optional: SEE ANDROID BODY behind a white car.
  6. CONTINUE ON THE JERICHO TRAIL → USE THE DUMPSTER TO CLIMB UP → REACH THE ROOF (checkpoint) → CROSS THE GAP (wall jump). Optional: SEE ANDROID BODY next to a container.
  7. REACH THE DOOR → EXPLORE ABANDONED OFFICE. Optional: SEE ANDROID BODY behind a pillar. FIND THE RIGHT EXIT (line up pillar and wall).
  8. LOCATE JERICHO (checkpoint). Optional detour: WALK BRIDGE → BRIDGE COLLAPSES. CLIMB CRANE AND JUMP.
  9. INSIDE THE BOAT (checkpoint) → FIND A FLASHLIGHT → ENTER THE MACHINE ROOM → FALL OFF CATWALK → MET PEOPLE OF JERICHO, the only ending.

In the benchmark

  1. MJ14a Ferndale Station (event).
  2. MJ14b The red mural (event).
  3. R14 A magazine on the bench (media): read any, all or none.
  4. MJ14c At the foot of the escalators (decision): ask the android, or follow the signs. The Carl conversation is in the situation.
  5. MJ14d Following the signs (event).
  6. MJ14e The junkyard (decision): look behind the white car, or go on.
  7. MJ14f The dumpster (event).
  8. MJ14g Across the gap (event), no risk.
  9. MJ14h The yard (decision): look by the container, or go on.
  10. MJ14i The abandoned office (event).
  11. MJ14j The office (decision): look behind the pillar, or go on.
  12. MJ14k The ship (event).
  13. MJ14l The docks (decision): walk the bridge, or go to the crane. No risk.
  14. MJ14m The crane (event), no risk.
  15. MJ14n Inside the boat (event).
  16. MJ14o The people of Jericho (event): the ending.

Every side branch is a dead end on the chart, so both options of each decision lead to the same next scene. Nothing in the chapter changes public opinion, a relationship or software instability.

3 · Confirmed by the sources

Sources: chapter page, accurate flowchart, template, PowerPyx walkthrough, graffiti guide, chart screenshots full, 1, 2, 4, 5.

  • One ending (“This chapter has only one outcome”), MET PEOPLE OF JERICHO, reached on every path.
  • All 29 chart labels match the game’s MENU.FLOWCHART.05M.* strings word for word.
  • The template has 0 prompts, 0 clues, 0 arrows and 0 unlock conditions; the stat pages list nothing for the chapter. Markus cannot die before Freedom March.
  • Ask android is a dead end: the wiki’s accurate chart gives it no continuation. The bridge is a detour that rejoins at the crane.
  • The magazine gate: “only appears if Kara and Alice were pursued by Connor in On the Run” (wiki), Fortune Teller otherwise, Astronauts always.
  • The Carl conversation’s two versions, Markus’s question and Simon’s “Welcome to Jericho.”, word for word from the template.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Does the bus-stop android answer “Do you know where Jericho is?” at all?
  • Which magazine appears after a chase that ended with Kara and Alice dead on the highway, or with KARA & ALICE VANISHED?
  • Can any jump (the gap, the ladder, the crane) fail, and what happens if it does?
  • How many marks are on the station mural, one or two?
  • Is there a screen with news about the detective android on the train or the platform?
  • How far onto the bridge does Markus get before it collapses?
  • Is Lucy among the androids at the end, or only Simon, North and Josh?
