1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. November 5, 2038, 03:24 PM: Kara reboots on display in Android Zone. Her system reports a memory reset.
  2. A girl and her mother leave. Kara can look around with the right stick but cannot walk. Two sales conversations can be overheard.
  3. Todd collects her from a human employee. He claims a car hit her; the employee explains that repairs required wiping her memory.
  4. Todd says his daughter chose her name. The employee orders name registration; Todd says Kara and she repeats her name.
  5. The opening credits follow the pickup through Detroit to Todd’s home. Play next switches to Markus in Shades of Color; Kara resumes in A New Home.

In the benchmark

  1. K00, Reboot in the store: the reset, girl, collection, damage claim and name registration. Continue takes Kara to Todd’s pickup.
  2. K00b, The drive home: the welcome sign, city, construction, advertising, coexistence and unemployment, then arrival. Continue completes the chapter.

Both scenes are type: "event" with empty option arrays; the controller supplies Continue. No choice branch, risk roll, requirement or stat delta is authored.

The five authored facts carry Kara’s prior ownership, damage claim, memory wipe, daughter-chosen name and observations of the city into her later knowledge. They are story knowledge, not relationship points.
