The Painter: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots); prompts and lines come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template and string table); the Fandom wiki’s chapter page and transcript fill in the rest. The chapter has no published number of any kind and no stat arrow; section 4 lists every simplification.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. ARRIVING HOME (checkpoint), 09:58: “Alarm deactivated. Welcome home, Markus.” DROP OFF PACKAGE on the hutch.
  2. Optional, any time before the pastime: the magazine in front of the TV (READ “NORTH POLE RUSSIA”, READ “LIFE ON TITAN”) and ACTIVATE BIRDS.
  3. WAKE CARL: curtains, weather, the injection (“Humans are such a fragile machine…”).
  4. CARL NOTICES MARKUS’ CLOTHES, only if the protesters harassed him in Shades of Color.
  5. SERVE BREAKFAST: bath, the agenda (retrospective, no news from Leo), bacon and eggs, “Television.” The CTN news on the Arctic plays (not on the chart).
  6. FIND SOMETHING TO DO (checkpoint): PLAY CHESS (WIN / LOSE / DRAW, each with a reply prompt), READ BOOK (Plato, Shakespeare, Keats) or PLAY PIANO (four moods).
  7. Carl: “One day, I won’t be here to take care of you anymore…” TAKE CARL TO THE STUDIO; Carl finishes his canvas; the verdict prompt LIKE / DON’T LIKE / NO OPINION (not on the chart).
  8. PAINT SOMETHING YOU SEE: Carl’s painting, the desk or a statue. “That is a perfect copy, of reality…”
  9. PAINT FROM THE HEART: ANDROIDS, HUMANITY or IDENTITY, then one of four feelings each.
  10. LEO INTRUDED TO ASK FOR MONEY: the only ending.

In the benchmark

  1. M01a Welcome home (event): both start nodes; the mirror shows the torn shirt when clothesTorn.
  2. R05 The magazine in the living room (media); M01b The birds in the hall (decision, optional).
  3. M01c Waking Carl (event).
  4. M01d Carl notices your clothes (event), only when clothesTorn.
  5. M01e Breakfast (event); R05b The news at breakfast (media: the TV, plus any cover left unread).
  6. M01f Something to do; then M01g Speed chess with M01h / M01i / M01j, or M01k The bookshelf, or M01l The piano.
  7. M01m The studio (event); M01n Carl’s painting (decision).
  8. M01o Paint something you see (decision).
  9. M01p Paint from the heart; then M01q Androids, M01r Humanity or M01s Identity.
  10. M01t Leo (event), every path; sets painterOutcome.

Nothing in the chapter changes public opinion, a relationship or any other hidden stat: the template has no arrow and Markus has no Carl relationship. Carl’s key lines and Markus’s painting are added to what Markus knows, which every later Markus scene shows him.

3 · Confirmed by the sources

Sources: chapter page, transcript, template, PowerPyx walkthrough, chart screenshots 1, 2, 3, 4.

  • One ending (“This chapter has only one outcome”), LEO INTRUDED TO ASK FOR MONEY, with no icon.
  • All 44 chart nodes, their order and their nesting; the piano order is the game’s choice group (MELANCHOLIC, HOPEFUL, INTIMATE, ENIGMATIC).
  • No arrow in the template; no probability, relationship, public-opinion or instability change in any source. Markus’s tracked relationships are Jericho, North, Josh and Simon only.
  • Every prompt the template ships: WIN / LOSE / DRAW, JUSTIFY / SORRY, LIE / TRUTH (twice), LIKE / DON’T LIKE / NO OPINION. The FACTUAL / REASSURING reply about the clothes is marked cut.
  • CARL NOTICES MARKUS’ CLOTHES needs PROTESTERS HARASS MARKUS in Shades of Color (template condition, wiki, PowerPyx).
  • Night of the Soul (Carl alive) reads the pastime (chessboard, books, piano) and shows Markus’s painting in the studio.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether doing nothing at FIND SOMETHING TO DO leaves all three pastime nodes grey and still reaches TAKE CARL TO THE STUDIO.
  • The on-screen order of the book and copy options, if any; and the order of the heart sub-choices.
  • Whether the magazine can still be read after breakfast, and whether ACTIVATE BIRDS can be done after waking Carl.
  • Whether the painting shown in Night of the Soul is the heart painting only, or also the copy.
  • The legend: the triangle on CARL NOTICES MARKUS’ CLOTHES and the bracketed icon on the side group.
