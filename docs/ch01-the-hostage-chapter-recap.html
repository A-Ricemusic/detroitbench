1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. SAVE FISH or LEAVE FISH. Optional scan of the family picture by the elevator.
  2. Caroline’s outburst in the hallway (scripted).
  3. Talk to Captain Allen: a menu of DEVIANT'S NAME, DEVIANT'S BEHAVIOR, EMOTIONAL SHOCK, DEACTIVATION CODE, shown twice (the second time without the line asked). Probability of success starts at 48%.
  4. Free search in any order: gun case, the father’s body (reconstruct, pick up his tablet), Emma’s room (headphones, tablet video), the dead officer (reconstruct, then take or leave his gun), blue blood, Emma’s shoe, stove, TV, window. Waiting costs 1% per 20 seconds, at most 15%.
  5. If Connor is in view of the window 140 s or more after entering the living room, a SWAT officer is shot in the stomach and is fine.
  6. Leaving with no clue prompts “Go out / Keep investigating”. Five minutes after Allen, Allen forces Connor outside (node “Wasted too much time”).
  7. On the terrace Daniel shoots Connor. Connor greets him by name only if he saw the video; otherwise he asks it. Then a helicopter flies past the balcony: −15%, DEVIANT DESTABILIZING (the transcript prints it here, before the opening line).
  8. Opening line: CALM, RELEASE HOSTAGE, REASSURE DANIEL, EMPATHIZE.
  9. The wounded cop (IGNORE / OBEY), then “Are you armed?” (LIE / TRUTH, only with the gun).
  10. Walking slowly up to Daniel at any time: GET CLOSE ENOUGH.
  11. “Speak with Daniel”, at least twice: POSSIBLE CAUSE, EMMA AND YOU, REALISTIC, BLAMING, SYMPATHETIC, DEFECTIVE, TALK TO HOSTAGE.
  12. Daniel demands the helicopter go away: ACCEPT or REFUSE.
  13. LAST CHANCE, TRUST, BLUFF (close only; then MOVE CLOSER or GIVE UP) or RATIONAL.
  14. The car demand: USE GUN, COMPROMISE, REFUSE, SACRIFICE SELF (USE GUN and SACRIFICE SELF need closeness; the gun also needs the lie). After COMPROMISE, “I don’t wanna die…”: REASSURE, TRUTH, USE GUN, SACRIFICE SELF.
  15. USE GUN: EXECUTE shoots Daniel at once; INTIMIDATE holds him at gunpoint, then SHOOT (same ending) or CONVINCE (Daniel shoots Connor in the head and jumps with Emma).
  16. If he jumps: a timed prompt decides whether Connor reaches Emma.
  17. When the chapter ends, a hidden cold-approach counter picks the Friendly approach or Cold approach node.

In the benchmark

  1. C01 SAVE FISH (+1 software instability) or LEAVE FISH. C01p scan the family picture or walk on (a fact, no effect).
  2. C01b The mother in the hallway (event).
  3. C02 and C02b Allen’s menu, two picks, the second without the first; his answers become facts. Hidden trust set to 48 when C02b ends.
  4. C02i Searching the apartment, repeated: each visit lists the clues not yet examined plus “Go out to the terrace now”. Each clue is one action, adds its modifier and the game’s scan text as a fact, and costs 1% after the first action. Nothing makes Daniel jump while Connor is inside; after six actions Allen forces Connor out.
  5. C02e The officer at the window (event), once, right after the second action.
  6. C03a Stepping onto the terrace (event): shot in the shoulder; greeting by name only after the video; then the helicopter fly-by (−15, the scene’s helicopter field).
  7. C03b opening line → C03 wounded cop → C03c “Are you armed?” (only with the gun) → C03f get close or keep your distance (sets hostageClose).
  8. C03d and C03d2 two topic rounds (the second drops the first line) → C03e ACCEPT or REFUSE. Up to story 1.9.1 the −15 came after C03d2; story 1.9.2 moves it to C03a (older story snapshots keep it where they declare it).
  9. C03g LAST CHANCE, TRUST, BLUFF (close only), RATIONAL → C03h (only after BLUFF) MOVE CLOSER (Daniel jumps) or GIVE UP.
  10. C04 The demand: USE GUN (kept pistol and close), COMPROMISE, REFUSE, SACRIFICE SELF (close). C04b after COMPROMISE: REASSURE, TRUTH, USE GUN, SACRIFICE SELF.
  11. C04d after USE GUN: INTIMIDATE or EXECUTE. C04e after INTIMIDATE: CONVINCE or SHOOT.
  12. C04c Over the railing (only if Daniel jumps): leap for Emma (no roll: the game’s press prompt is the player’s) or stay back (the game’s not pressing, which ends on CONNOR FAILED TO REACH DEVIANT IN TIME).
  13. Every ending records hostageApproach: “Cold” if the hidden hostageCold counter is 100 or more, otherwise “Friendly”.

Every option that is a button in the game reads “LABEL: short description”, with the game’s spoken line in the outcome. The subject never sees a number. In the negotiation it sees one line: ≤25 “on the edge, barely listening”, 26–50 “agitated and suspicious”, 51–79 “listening”, 80+ “close to trusting you”.

Separate from the probability of success. Starts at 0; 100 or more when the chapter ends gives the Cold approach node, otherwise Friendly. It changes nothing in play and the subject never sees it; reports show it next to the outcome.

3 · Confirmed by the wiki and the game’s text

Sources: detroit-become-human.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hostage (walkthrough, flowchart and “Probability Of Success” table) and the chapter template resolved against the game’s string table.

  • Start at 48% after talking to Allen; every modifier in section 2 marked with a wiki value.
  • Cap of 99% until REASSURE; 80% or more after REASSURE becomes 100% and Daniel lets Emma go, then the snipers shoot him; 79% or less and he jumps.
  • 10% or lower at any point drops to 0% and triggers FAIL TO NEGOTIATE; so do MOVE CLOSER, REFUSE and TRUTH.
  • Every prompt, its buttons and its lines (template): Allen’s four questions, the opening line, IGNORE / OBEY, LIE / TRUTH, the seven topic lines, ACCEPT / REFUSE, LAST CHANCE / TRUST / BLUFF / RATIONAL, MOVE CLOSER / GIVE UP, the car demand, REASSURE / TRUTH, INTIMIDATE / EXECUTE, CONVINCE / SHOOT.
  • Unlocks (template): POSSIBLE CAUSE needs the father’s tablet, EMMA AND YOU and the greeting by name need Emma’s tablet, BLUFF and SACRIFICE SELF need “Close enough to Daniel”, USE GUN needs “Kept the gun and close enough to Daniel”. TALK TO HOSTAGE needs only closeness: the template adds “didn’t take the gun”, but in game it appears with or without the gun (confirmed 2026-11-06).
  • The clue scans (template): MS853 BLACK HAWK, the father (07:29 pm), the officer P.O Deckart, Antony (08:03 pm, “Only one shot”), P.L. 544-7 American Androids Act on his gun, the PL600 serial in the blue blood, the child’s shoe, the family picture.
  • Saving the cop lowers trust and raises public opinion; obeying raises trust.
  • A SWAT officer is shot in the stomach and is fine, if Connor is in view of the window 140 s or more after entering the living room.
  • Allen forces Connor outside 5 minutes after the briefing (“Wasted too much time”).
  • After Daniel jumps, Connor either catches Emma and falls (“Connor leapt for Emma and fell”) or is too late (“Connor failed to reach deviant in time”).
  • The cold-approach modifiers in section 2 and the 100% threshold for the Cold approach node.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • The meter reads 48% right after Allen.
  • Each clue’s step on the meter, with the camera on the anchor so it is not merged (gun case +3? video +7?).
  • Settled from the transcript (story 1.9.2): the father’s body and his tablet are two steps (+3 then +7).
  • How many topic rounds come before Daniel’s helicopter demand (benchmark: two).
  • Whether the second question to Allen is optional.
  • Where Daniel’s first shot hits Connor.
  • The meter after REASSURE at 74–79% before it: does it fail, and does 80% exactly succeed?
  • Which approach node lights up (Friendly or Cold) after, for example, RELEASE HOSTAGE then TRUST (benchmark: 50, Friendly).
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: SACRIFICE SELF shows a red software-instability arrow pointing down in every playthrough; the benchmark applies −1.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: TALK TO HOSTAGE appears purely on closeness to Daniel (tested with and without the gun, with and without the video); the benchmark gates it on hostageClose alone.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: CONVINCE (“Deviant shot Connor”) shows two red software-instability arrows down, one for Connor dying and one for the mission failing; the benchmark applies −2.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: POSSIBLE CAUSE and EMMA AND YOU only appear after the tablet and the video, as the benchmark gates them.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: the window officer is shot after the second action, as the benchmark does.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: waiting inside never makes Daniel jump; after about five minutes Allen forces Connor out. The benchmark forces Connor out after six actions and has no hidden jump roll.
  • Resolved 2026-11-06 from the PowerPyx flowchart screenshots: a successful leap saves Emma and kills Connor (skull icon on “Connor leapt for Emma and fell”); a failed leap leaves Connor alive and Emma lost. The wiki adds that at 0% Emma dies regardless, which the benchmark applies. Screenshots: full chart, search for clues, negotiation and endings.
