# Chapter 2: "The Opening" (Kara)

Wiki title: `The Opening` (the title `Opening` does not exist on the wiki; the API returns `missingtitle`). PowerPyx and IGN call the chapter "Opening". In-game chapter date and time: "NOV 5TH, 2038 / PM 03:24". Playable character: Kara. Location: Android Zone store, Detroit, then the drive to Todd Williams' house (opening credits).

**Summary: this chapter has no flowchart, no player decision, no stat change and one outcome.** All three primary sources agree:

- Wiki infobox: `|objectives=None` and `|endings=1`; lead: "This chapter has only one outcome."
- Wiki Notes: "This is the only chapter of the game that does not have a flowchart."
- PowerPyx: "The next chapter doesn't have any flowchart tasks at all. After The Hostage chapter you will see a nice cutscene of Kara and Todd driving to their house. After that cutscene the 3rd chapter "Shades of Color" begins."

The only interaction is looking around with the right stick (transcript: "TUTORIAL LOOK AROUND USING THE RIGHT STICK"), which lets Kara optionally overhear two sales conversations.

## 4. Cross-chapter dependencies

**Into this chapter:** none. No earlier choice changes anything here.

**Out of this chapter:** no flag is set, because there is no choice. The chapter does establish story facts that later Kara chapters build on:

- **Memory wipe.** Dan: "Except that we had to reset it. Meaning we had to wipe its memory." In A New Home, Kara can tell Alice "I know I used to live here, but they erased my memory..." (Kara page quote, attributed to A New Home). A New Home's Relationship Changes table includes "Asking Alice to help jog her memory when talking to her in her room {{Icon|up}}" and "Kara optimistically suspects they used to be friends once before offering to be friends again ... {{Icon|up}}". Those Alice relationship rises belong to A New Home, not to this chapter.
- **Todd's lie about the damage.** Todd: "Huh, A car hit it… Stupid accident…" A New Home overview: "She was allegedly hit by a car, but depending on the player's choices, she may learn that she was actually broken by [[Todd Williams|Todd]]." Todd page: "He destroyed [[Kara]] multiple times before the start of the game, who was repaired and sent back reset with a memory wipe." Stormy Night: Todd threatens to "bust her worse than last time" (Todd page, personality section).
- **The name came from Alice.** Todd: "My daughter did…" A New Home Relationship Changes: "Approving of the name Alice gave her when talking to Alice in her room {{Icon|up}}".
- **Zoe's model.** The Zoe page notes the same character model hugs a black AX400 at the start of Shades of Color and asks about the android Mandy in Jericho, but it is not certain this is the same character. This is cosmetic.
