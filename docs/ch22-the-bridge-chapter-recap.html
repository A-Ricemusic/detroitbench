The Bridge: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 17 nodes); prompts, labels and stat arrows from the detroitbecometext template and string table (12 prompts, 41 options, 29 arrows); the outcome rule and the death’s effect from the Fandom wiki (chapter page, Relationship Stat, Software Instability, Hank and Connor pages). There is no probability, timer or public-opinion change. Everything that matters is Hank’s relationship, which decides whether he shoots. Specification: docs/research/ch22-the-bridge.md.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. AMBASSADOR BRIDGE (checkpoint) → LEAVE CAR. November 7, 2038, 01:19AM, Riverside Park.
  2. Two magazines on the bench ahead: READ “MYSTERIOUS KAMSKI”, READ “MARKETS PREDICT WAR”. Only before talking to Hank.
  3. TALK TO HANK: “Nice view, huh?” PERSONAL QUESTION opens PHOTO / SUICIDE (DISCUSS PHOTO, DISCUSS RUSSIAN ROULETTE) if Connor saw Cole’s photo; otherwise the SUICIDE line plays.
  4. The investigation: rA9 (DISCUSS RA9, locked on earlier rA9 clues), SHOCK, SYSTEMS, SOFTWARE.
  5. The Tracis, in one of two variants (found, or not found).
  6. “What are you really?”
  7. Hank’s question about the Tracis’ fate, in one of four variants (spared, shot in the back, shot one, not found).
  8. HANK DRAWS GUN (“I could kill you... And you would just come back” if Connor died before).
  9. “Are you afraid to die?”: SHOW FEAR, SHOW NO FEAR, REMAIN LOGICAL, BE COLD.
  10. “What will happen if I pull this trigger?”
  11. HANK IS PLACATED → HANK LEFT CONNOR ALONE, or HANK IS HOSTILE → HANK SHOT CONNOR (skull).

In the benchmark

  1. C13a, a narrated event.
  2. R22, a media scene with both magazines.
  3. C13b, then C13c when the photo was seen (knowsCole).
  4. C13d; rA9 is shown only if Connor met rA9 earlier.
  5. C13e (found) or C13f (not found).
  6. C13g.
  7. C13h (spared), C13i (shot in the back), C13j (shot one) or C13k (not found).
  8. C13l, a narrated event; the line reads connorDeaths.
  9. C13, four answers.
  10. C13m, four answers.
  11. C13n or C13o, events gated on Hank’s level after C13m.

4 · The ending

Hank shoots only when his level is Hostile (LEVELS.hank, at or below −4) after the last answer (C13m), so DEFY can tip a Tense Hank into shooting. His level is cumulative across the playthrough: no single answer decides it. From Warm or better no path through this chapter reaches Hostile. HANK SHOT CONNOR goes through connorDies with this chapter’s death effect (Hank −1 only); Connor is replaced and keeps his memory.

6 · Where the benchmark simplifies

  • The rA9 lock’s clues are unspecified in every source; the benchmark accepts any earlier rA9 knowledge.
  • Which of NOT ALIVE and LOGICAL lights REMAIN LOGICAL and which BE COLD is unsettled; the benchmark follows the string keys (only the recorded label differs).
  • The chart’s direct line from the answers to HANK LEFT CONNOR ALONE has no stated condition; both placated nodes play together.
  • The not-found variant covers every MISSION FAILED ending at the club.
  • Timeouts on the dialogue rounds are not modelled (no source).

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether Hank’s level is read after the trigger round (DEFY can tip it) or when he draws the gun.
  • Whether TRUTH raises Hank once or twice.
  • Whether the not-found COLD moves instability.
  • Whether Hank’s “come back” line needs one earlier death or two.
  • Whether the shooting changes instability.
  • Which earlier clues unlock rA9.
  • Which answer lights REMAIN LOGICAL and which BE COLD.
