1 · How the chapter flows

Where Kara slept in Fugitives picks the start. The car and the motel merge into the street; the street, the motel’s side door and the squat merge into the pursuit.

Parking lot (car)

  1. K09l0 MORNING IN PARKING LOT, LEAVE ALICE ASLEEP IN CAR (event).
  2. K09l EXPLORE PARKING LOT (repeatable): scissors (then K09l1 BROWN / BLACK / WHITE / BLONDE and REMOVE LED), burnt android, two magazines, TRY TO OPEN CAR TRUNK > FIND A CROWBAR > FIND SOME CLOTHES (only without the laundromat clothes), CHECK NEIGHBORHOOD, WAKE UP ALICE (needs the haircut and the look outside).
  3. C09s CONNOR INVESTIGATES SUPERMARKET, POLICE DON’T HAVE A LEAD (event).

Motel

  1. K09m0 MORNING IN MOTEL (event). K09m1 TALK TO ALICE: BATH? / HUNGRY? / TIRED?
  2. K09m the room (repeatable): DRAW A BATH (then K09m2 the hair), clock, window, painting, two magazines, FINISH DRAWING BATH.
  3. K09m3 CHANGE CHANNEL, ALICE HEARS ABOUT TODD (event; public opinion −1).
  4. K09m4 CAN’T STAY HERE / FAMILY / CONTACT ANDROID. K09m5 CHEER ALICE UP: MOVING / DETERMINED / COMFORTING / SUPPORTIVE.
  5. K09m6 LOOK FOR BREAKFAST, KARA SPOTS POLICE FORCES (event).
  6. C09m0 the desk (event). C09m1 “You wait in the car.”: RATIONAL / DIPLOMATIC / DIRECT. C09m2 FOLLOW HANK (“It’s gone…”) or CHECK THE DOOR (“Found you.”, the chase).

The street (car; motel after FOLLOW HANK)

  1. K09s KARA & ALICE IN THE STREET, WALK TOWARD TRAIN STATION (event).
  2. K09 SNEAK PAST POLICE FORCES (risk 0.25) or walk in the open (always spotted).
  3. K09t REACHED THE TRAIN SAFELY, or K09r SPOTTED BY POLICE, KARA & ALICE RUN AWAY (the chase).

Squat

  1. K09q0 MORNING IN SQUAT (event). K09q EXPLORE SQUAT (repeatable): two magazines, REVIVE FIRE (needed to go up), READ LEAFLET, CHECK ON ALICE, LOOK AT RALPH’S STUFF (then K09q1 STEAL / PUT BACK), GO UPSTAIRS.
  2. K09u upstairs (repeatable): CUT AND COLOR HAIR (then K09u1), FIND CORPSE IN BATHTUB, CHANGE CLOTHES, TAKE GUN BACK, GO DOWNSTAIRS (leaving the gun is FORGOT GUN).
  3. C09q0 POLICE ARRIVES: Connor and Hank across the street (event, wire-cutters variant).
  4. K09q2 RALPH WANTS A FAMILY MEAL (event). K09q3 SIT / REFUSE, K09q4 SIT / REFUSE again. K09q5 [CHECK GUN] / WORRIED / EXPLAIN / AGREE. K09q6 LIE / TRUTH.
  5. K09q7–K09q9 the table: [ASK ABOUT CORPSE] / [INSIST ABOUT CORPSE] / THREATEN / BLUFF / FAMILY, asked again after ASK or BLUFF. K09q10 DISTURBED BY CONNOR (event): flee by the back door after a threat, otherwise hide under the stairs.
  6. C09q1 CONNOR INVESTIGATES SQUAT, ENTER SQUAT (event). C09a INTERROGATE: AX400 / DEVIANTS / REASSURE / BLUE BLOOD (+ PRESSURIZE after a threat; then Ralph informs).
  7. C09q INVESTIGATE SQUAT GROUND FLOOR (repeatable): CHECK UPSTAIRS, ANALYZE, TABLE, UNDER THE STAIRS, KITCHEN, FIREPLACE, LEAVE THE SQUAT. C09q2 found under the stairs (chase), or C09q3 SQUAT ANDROID DISAPPEARS and K09q11 KARA & ALICE UNDISCOVERED IN SQUAT.

Connor pursues Kara

  1. C09p CONNOR PURSUES KARA (event). C09r RUN: stay (C09v KARA & ALICE VANISHED), run partway or run too slowly (C09x KARA & ALICE CROSSED THE HIGHWAY), or run hard (risk 0.2) and REACH FENCE IN TIME.
  2. K09h KARA & ALICE ARE AT THE HIGHWAY (event). K09h1 first lanes (risk 0.1; failure: CAR HITS KARA & ALICE).
  3. C09 HANK ORDERS CONNOR NOT TO GO: GIVE UP or GO.
  4. GIVE UP: K09h2 last lanes (0.1). GO: C09h1 Connor’s first lanes (0.2; failure CAR HITS CONNOR, then K09h4 Kara’s last lanes, 0.1); success: CONNOR CATCHES KARA, K09h3 the fight (0.1; failure CAR HITS KARA), C09h2 CONNOR AVOIDS COLLISION or CONNOR HIT BY CAR (0.2).
  5. K09e1 / K09e2 KARA & ALICE REACHED THE OTHER SIDE (Connor standing / Connor destroyed); C09d KARA & ALICE DIED ON THE HIGHWAY from Connor’s side (not played when all three died).

3 · Confirmed by the chart and the game’s text

  • Every prompt label and order, from the template: BATH? / HUNGRY? / TIRED?; CAN’T STAY HERE / FAMILY / CONTACT ANDROID; MOVING / DETERMINED / COMFORTING / SUPPORTIVE; RATIONAL / DIPLOMATIC / DIRECT; SIT / REFUSE twice; [CHECK GUN] / WORRIED / EXPLAIN / AGREE; LIE / TRUTH; [ASK ABOUT CORPSE] / [INSIST ABOUT CORPSE] / THREATEN / BLUFF / FAMILY; AX400 / DEVIANTS / REASSURE / BLUE BLOOD (/ PRESSURIZE); GO / GIVE UP. The hair colors and STEAL / PUT BACK are from the chart and the string table.
  • The scan of Ralph (PROCESSING LED, MODEL WR600 - Gardener, BURN MARK) and both TV reports are quoted from the game’s text. Neither report mentions Alice.
  • THREATEN is always offered: with the knife on the table, or the gun if Kara has it (template variants, the Ralph page). It is this threat that makes Ralph tell Connor “She ran out the back door!”.
  • Ralph defends Kara only if Connor checks under the stairs; that is what lets him return in Battle for Detroit (ralphDefended).

4 · Adapted or not in the sources

  • Free-roam phases are repeatable scenes: the parking lot, the motel room, the squat’s ground floor and upstairs, and Connor’s search. Leaving needs what the game makes unmissable (the haircut, CHECK NEIGHBORHOOD, REVIVE FIRE).
  • Gameplay checks with a failure branch on the chart are risk rolls: the street sneak (0.25), the run to the fence (0.2), Kara’s three crossings and the fight (0.1 each), Connor’s two (0.2 each). All values are unsourced. Experienced difficulty is modelled.
  • Connor’s search: Hank calls after three empty looks and orders Connor out after the fifth. PowerPyx only says “if you wait for a long time”.
  • CHANGE CHANNEL is unavoidable (no bypass edge on the chart), so the motel start always costs public opinion.
  • [CHECK GUN] is shown whenever Kara started with the gun. If she left it upstairs, the outcome says so. This follows PowerPyx and the wiki over the template’s “didn’t forget it upstairs”.
  • TRY TO CONVINCE RALPH is read as FAMILY: only FAMILY and INSIST lead to the apology in the template. PowerPyx calls it BLUFF.
  • LEAVE THE SQUAT (string table) fills the chart’s unlabelled box between the search and SQUAT ANDROID DISAPPEARS.
  • Not built: the cut prompt REASSURING / ANALYTICAL / VULNERABLE / SINCERE; the string table’s unplaced FEEL? button; the hair CHANGE / FINISH retry; Hank’s line after a quiet escape in the car row. The leaflet’s text is unknown.
  • Old bugs fixed: walking openly in the street no longer kills Kara and Alice on the freeway (it always leads to the chase). A highway death no longer leads to an “escape” text. The squat search is under the stairs, not upstairs, and has no risk.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Does any Hank or instability arrow show at the motel’s “You wait in the car.”, at GIVE UP / GO, and when Connor is hit? The template shows none; the wiki shows all of them.
  • Is CHANGE CHANNEL avoidable? Can Kara leave the motel without it?
  • Is [CHECK GUN] shown when the gun was forgotten upstairs? Does it need two refusals?
  • Which table line is TRY TO CONVINCE RALPH on the chart: FAMILY or BLUFF? Does BLUFF bring the menu back?
  • What is the red-padlock box between INVESTIGATE SQUAT GROUND FLOOR and SQUAT ANDROID DISAPPEARS? Is it LEAVE THE SQUAT?
  • How many looks around the squat before Hank calls, and before he orders Connor out?
  • Which highway deaths are possible on Casual? (Experienced is modelled.)
  • Does a gun threat on Ralph in Fugitives also make him inform here? (Not modelled: only the table threat does.)
