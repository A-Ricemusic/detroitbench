Russian Roulette: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (PowerPyx 100% screenshots, 30 nodes); prompts, button labels, lines, clue text and stat arrows come from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext template and string table, docs/research/ch18-russian-roulette.md section 10); PowerPyx and the Fandom wiki fill in the rest. The chapter has no probability, public-opinion or instability change, no risk roll, and one ending. Connor cannot die in it.

1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. EMERGE IN THE ZEN GARDEN (checkpoint) → JOIN AMANDA: her verdict on The Nest (and on Partners), then up to four rounds of questions.
  2. ARRIVE AT HANK’S PLACE (checkpoint) by taxi. [ FIND LT. ANDERSON ]: ring, ring again, knock, the car, three windows, all optional → SPOT HANK AND BREAK IN at the kitchen window.
  3. ENTER HOUSE (checkpoint) → HANK’S DOG ATTACKS → CALM HANK’S DOG if Connor learned Sumo’s name in Waiting for Hank…, otherwise RILE HANK’S DOG.
  4. ANALYZE HANK (four scans) → TAKE HANK TO THE BATHROOM (the shower).
  5. RATIONAL = UNDERSTANDING ATTITUDE; AGGRESSIVE, SOLUTION, PRAGMATIC = DISTANT ATTITUDE. Then TEASE or LEAVE (off the chart).
  6. TAKE HIPPY / STREAKY / STRIPY SHIRT.
  7. [ WAIT FOR HANK ], about three minutes: two magazines, the TV, FIND PICTURE OF HANK’S SON (cross-chapter), LEARN HANK’S INTERESTS, PET HANK’S DOG, the window apology, the gun (QUESTION → LEARN HANK WAS PLAYING RUSSIAN ROULETTE).
  8. HANK IS READY → LEFT FOR EDEN CLUB, the only ending.

In the benchmark

  1. C11z The garden (event): Amanda’s reaction and arrow. C11a PRAGMATIC / EXPLAIN / NO EXCUSE (Rupert not caught). C11b1, C11b2 what Connor learned, two answers. C11c the self-destruct (Rupert caught). C11d the lieutenant, then Amanda’s closing lines.
  2. C11e Arrival (event). C11f the checks outside, repeated; the kitchen window ends it.
  3. C11g The dog (event), CALM or RILE by knowsSumo.
  4. C11h the four scans and the dog, repeated; waking Hank ends it. C11i the bathroom (event).
  5. C11j the shower round. C11k TEASE / LEAVE.
  6. C11l the shirts.
  7. R18 the magazines and the TV (media). C11m the house, repeated; the gun or waiting ends it. C11n QUESTION / SAY NOTHING, only after the gun.
  8. C11o Hank is ready (event), every path.

No option carries a risk. Engine logic lives in benchmark/chapters/ch18.mjs; everything else is data in benchmark/chapters/ch18.json.

7 · What to check when you replay

  • Which action brings Hank out: the gun, the photo, or both; and whether other things can be looked at after the gun.
  • Whether Hank drops on the bathroom when Connor survived The Nest.
  • Whether Amanda drops when Rupert got away but the Partners deviant was found.
  • Whether Connor gives a second answer to “What else?” when only BIRDS is unlocked.
  • Whether NEGATIVE / AMBIGUOUS / POSITIVE depend on Hank’s relationship level.
  • Whether a scan is required before Connor can wake Hank.
  • Whether ADVICE is ever offered in the shower, and whether SOLUTION gets a reply.
  • Whether CALM HANK’S DOG needs the DOG exchange exhausted, not just asked.
