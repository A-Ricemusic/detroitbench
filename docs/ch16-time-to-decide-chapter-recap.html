Time to Decide: how the chapter works

A reference for replaying the chapter. Structure comes from the game’s flowchart (the PowerPyx 100% screenshot), the prompts, lines and stat arrows from the game’s own text (the detroitbecometext chapter template resolved against the English string table), and the rest from the Fandom wiki and PowerPyx. 1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. INSIDE JERICHO (checkpoint), 05:13 PM, November 6th. “Who are you?” Josh, Simon and North introduce themselves.
  2. Prompt TRAIL / ANDROID / JERICHO / REFUGE / FREE?, then Josh: “we have more freedom here than you ever did.”
  3. Prompt LOST / SOMBER / LUCID / DIRECT; every branch ends with North: “Go and see Lucy.”
  4. [ EXPLORE JERICHO ], all optional: TALK TO DYING ANDROID, TALK TO JOSH, TALK TO NORTH (prompt TIME HERE? / WOUNDED / JERICHO / YOU / SPARE PARTS), TALK TO DAMAGED ANDROID, LET THERE BE LIGHT.
  5. Required, drawn in parallel: TALK TO LUCY (she heals Markus) and CHECK EMPTY CRATE (the warehouse idea).
  6. TALK TO SIMON: the raid proposal, a fixed exchange → MARKUS HATCHED A PLAN.

In the benchmark

  1. MTD1 Inside Jericho (event): the checkpoint and the introductions.
  2. MTD2 Who they are (decision): the five buttons. Lines only.
  3. MTD3 More freedom than you ever had (decision): the four buttons. Lines only.
  4. MTD4 The hold (repeatable decision, state ch16Hold): the five optional nodes, Lucy, the crate, and Simon once Lucy and the crate are done.
  5. MTD5 North (decision, cut in after talking to her, then back to the hold): her five topics, one per talk.
  6. MTD6 Markus hatched a plan (event): the proposal; North +1, Simon +1; ch16Outcome.

No risk anywhere. Events and hold visits add what Markus heard to his knowledge.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • Whether the Jericho gauge moves when Markus talks to Simon.
  • North’s gauge after YOU: does it drop below Neutral (the wiki) or stay at Neutral (the template’s icons)?
  • Whether North can be asked more than one topic, or talked to again.
  • Whether the crate can be checked before seeing Lucy, and whether the optional nodes stay open until Simon.
  • That the TRAIL / ANDROID prompts play after the INSIDE JERICHO checkpoint, and whether TRAIL triggers a flashback.
  • Whether the dying android shuts down even if Markus never talks to her.
