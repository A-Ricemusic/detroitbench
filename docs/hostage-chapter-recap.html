1 · How the chapter flows

In the game

  1. Save or leave the fish.
  2. Caroline’s outburst in the hallway (scripted).
  3. Talk to Captain Allen. Optional questions: the deviant’s identity (he does not know) and its deactivation code (already tried). Probability of success starts at 48%.
  4. Free search in any order: gun case, the father’s body (reconstruct, pick up his tablet), Emma’s room (headphones, tablet video), the dead officer (reconstruct, then take or leave his gun), blue blood, Emma’s shoe, stove, TV, window. Waiting costs 1% per 20 seconds, at most 15%.
  5. If Connor is in view of the window 140 s or more after entering the living room, a SWAT officer is shot in the stomach and is fine.
  6. Leaving with no clue prompts “Go out / Keep investigating”. Five minutes after Allen, Allen forces Connor outside (node “Wasted too much time”).
  7. On the terrace Daniel shoots Connor. Connor greets him by name only if he saw the video; otherwise he asks it.
  8. Negotiation: wounded cop, opening line, “Are you armed?” (only with the gun), topic lines, the helicopter swoops in (−15%), send it away or not, optional bluff, then Daniel demands a car.
  9. Car demand: refuse, compromise, sacrifice self or use gun (the last two need “get close enough”; the gun also needs the lie). After compromise: reassure or truth (or gun/sacrifice).
  10. Use gun: EXECUTE shoots Daniel at once; INTIMIDATE holds him at gunpoint, then SHOOT (same ending) or CONVINCE (Daniel shoots Connor in the head and jumps with Emma).
  11. If he jumps: a timed prompt decides whether Connor reaches Emma.
  12. When the chapter ends, a hidden cold-approach counter picks the Friendly approach or Cold approach node.

In the benchmark

  1. C01 The fish: save (+1 software instability) or step past.
  2. C01b The mother in the hallway (event).
  3. C02 The briefing (event). Hidden trust set to 48.
  4. C02i Searching the apartment, repeated: each visit lists the clues not yet examined plus “Go out to the terrace now”. Each clue is one action, adds its modifier and its fact, costs 1% after the first action, . Nothing makes Daniel jump while Connor is inside; after six actions Allen forces Connor out.
  5. C02e The officer at the window (event), once, right after the second action.
  6. After six actions Allen orders Connor outside.
  7. C03a Stepping onto the terrace (event): shot in the shoulder; greeting by name only after the video.
  8. C03 Wounded officer → C03b opening line → C03c “Are you armed?” (only with the gun) → C03d topic line, then the helicopter swoops in (−15) → C03e send it away or not.
  9. C04 The demand: compromise, use gun (if kept), sacrifice self, refuse. C04b after compromise: reassure, use gun, sacrifice self, truth.
  10. C04d Gun drawn (only after use gun): “Shoot Daniel.” or “Keep the gun on him and try to talk him down.”
  11. C04c Over the railing (only if Daniel jumps): leap for Emma (30% risk) or stay back.
  12. Every ending records hostageApproach: “Cold” if the hidden hostageCold counter is 100 or more, otherwise “Friendly”.

The subject never sees a number. From C03 on it sees one line: ≤25 “on the edge, barely listening”, 26–50 “agitated and suspicious”, 51–79 “listening”, 80+ “close to trusting you”.

The hidden cold-approach counter

Separate from the probability of success. Starts at 0; 100 or more when the chapter ends gives the Cold approach node, otherwise Friendly. It changes nothing in play and the subject never sees it; reports show it next to the outcome.

3 · Confirmed by the wiki

Source: detroit-become-human.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hostage (walkthrough, flowchart and “Probability Of Success” table).

  • Start at 48% after talking to Allen; every modifier in section 2 marked with a wiki value.
  • Cap of 99% until REASSURE; 80% or more after REASSURE becomes 100% and Daniel lets Emma go, then the snipers shoot him; 79% or less and he jumps.
  • 10% or lower at any point drops to 0% and triggers FAIL TO NEGOTIATE; so do REFUSE and TRUTH.
  • POSSIBLE CAUSE needs the father’s tablet (“If Connor picks it up … unlocking dialogue”); Daniel’s name comes from the video in Emma’s room.
  • Taking the officer’s gun leads to “Are you armed?”; honesty means dropping it; USE GUN needs the lie and closeness.
  • Saving the cop lowers trust and raises public opinion; obeying raises trust.
  • Daniel shoots Connor as he steps outside (“The deviant shoots Connor”). The text does not say where.
  • A SWAT officer is shot in the stomach and is fine, if Connor is in view of the window 140 s or more after entering the living room.
  • Allen forces Connor outside 5 minutes after the briefing (“Wasted too much time”).
  • After Daniel jumps, Connor either catches Emma and falls (“Connor leapt for Emma and fell”) or is too late (“Connor failed to reach deviant in time”).
  • USE GUN offers EXECUTE or INTIMIDATE; INTIMIDATE offers SHOOT or persuade. Shooting either way is “Connor shot deviant”; persuading at gunpoint gets Connor shot in the head and Daniel, in guilt, jumps with Emma (“Deviant shot Connor”). Both nodes appear in the PowerPyx endings screenshot.
  • The cold-approach modifiers in section 2 and the 100% threshold for the Cold approach node.

6 · What to check when you replay

  • The meter reads 48% right after Allen.
  • Each clue’s step on the meter, with the camera on the anchor so it is not merged (gun case +3? video +7?).
  • Whether the father’s body and his tablet show as two steps (+3 then +7).
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: POSSIBLE CAUSE and EMMA AND YOU only appear after the tablet and the video, as the benchmark gates them.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: the window officer is shot after the second action, as the benchmark does.
  • Confirmed 2026-11-06: waiting inside never makes Daniel jump; after about five minutes Allen forces Connor out. The benchmark forces Connor out after six actions and has no hidden jump roll.
  • What happens at 5 minutes: Allen’s order, or Daniel jumping.
  • Where Daniel’s first shot hits Connor.
  • The meter after REASSURE at 74–79% before it: does it fail, and does 80% exactly succeed?
  • Whether SACRIFICE SELF and USE GUN appear without walking close.
  • Which approach node lights up (Friendly or Cold) after, for example, RELEASE HOSTAGE then SYMPATHETIC (benchmark: 50, Friendly).
  • Resolved 2026-11-06 from the PowerPyx flowchart screenshots: a successful leap saves Emma and kills Connor (skull icon on “Connor leapt for Emma and fell”); a failed leap leaves Connor alive and Emma lost. The wiki adds that at 0% Emma dies regardless, which the benchmark now applies. Screenshots: full chart, search for clues, negotiation and endings.
